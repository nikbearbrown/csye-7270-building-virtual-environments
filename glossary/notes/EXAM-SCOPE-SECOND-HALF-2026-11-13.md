**Input and output, as the exam would state them (Claude's wording, not yet checked by Bear):**
- Vertex shader. Input: one vertex (its 3D position, plus data such as UV and colour). Output: where that vertex lands on the 2D screen, plus values handed on to the fragment stage.
- Fragment (pixel) shader. Input: one screen position the shape covers, with values blended from the corners (UV, colour) and any textures. Output: the colour (and alpha) of that one pixel. It cannot see what colour its neighbours ended up with.

10. **FK vs IK wording.** "Pull the hand and the arm follows" is IK (inverse kinematics: you place the end, and the joints above it are worked out). FK (forward kinematics) is the other direction: you rotate each joint from the shoulder outward, and the hand ends up wherever that puts it. Exam statements will keep the two apart that way, at the "why add it" level: IK for reaching, planting feet and grabbing; FK for full control of an arc, like a swing. Retargeting: reusing one character's animation on a different skeleton. Confirm that's the level you want.
