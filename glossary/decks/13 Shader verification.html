Reading the output

Golden image, pixel diff and tolerance

Source: glossary, Shader verification (golden image, reference image, per-platform reference); Chapter 8, Unity and Unreal comparisons; Unity Graphics Test Framework manual.

Two 16 × 16 images from a seeded generator: noise of up to 2 per pixel plus a 3 × 3 defect of +45. Drag the slider to change the tolerance; counts are computed from the 256 pixels. Pixel diff is not in the chapters; the idea is image comparison against a reference in Chapter 8’s Unity and Unreal sections.
