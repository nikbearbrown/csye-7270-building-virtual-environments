Part one

Turn and move

Pan, tilt, roll, dolly, truck, pedestal, orbit

Seven terms for turning a camera or moving it.

Computed

Turning on the spot

Pan, tilt, roll

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Each camera turns and its position never changes. Pan is left and right, tilt is up and down, roll tips the horizon. In-view posts turn red, so you see what the turn brings into frame.

Computed

Moving the camera

Dolly, truck, pedestal

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

The camera moves and keeps facing the same way. Dolly is forward and back along the view, truck is sideways, pedestal is straight up and down. Distances are in metres at 100 px per metre.

Computed

Moving around a subject

Orbit

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Step one: the camera moves on a circle and stays aimed at the subject, so the distance stays 1.90 m. Step two: the same angle done as a pan on the spot loses the subject.

Part two

The lens

Zoom, focal length, field of view, dolly versus zoom, parallax, projection

Seven terms about what the lens does, and how it differs from moving.

Computed

The lens

Zoom, focal length, field of view

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

The camera never moves. Angle = 2 atan(18 / mm) for a 36 mm wide sensor, an assumption stated on the slide.

Computed

Look-alikes

Dolly versus zoom

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Scene numbers: house 3 m away, tower 9 m away, 240 px focal scale. A 1.2 m dolly gives the house x1.67 and the tower x1.15. A x1.67 zoom gives both x1.67. The size ratio house to tower changes only in the dolly.

Computed

Near against far

Parallax

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Shift in the picture = 220 px x 1 m / distance. Near 2 m: 110 px. Mid 4 m: 55 px. Far 8 m: 27.5 px.

Computed

Projection

Perspective and orthographic

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Perspective box width = 260 px / distance: 87, 58, 40, 29, 20 px. Orthographic: 44 px every time.

Part three

Following the action

Follow camera, smoothing, deadzone, look-ahead, screen shake, camera limits

Six terms about how the camera tracks a moving target.

Simulation

Following

Follow camera and smoothing

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Exponential smoothing with a 0.25 s time constant, stepped at 60 frames per second. Peak lag is computed from the simulation.

Simulation

Framing

Deadzone and look-ahead

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Deadzone half-width 70 px. Look-ahead: lead 0.5 s at 200 px/s gives 100 px, eased in with a 0.25 s time constant.

Simulation (seeded)

Impact

Screen shake

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Amplitude 18 px, decay constant 0.18 s, random direction each frame from a fixed seed so replays match. 5% is reached at ln(20) x 0.18 = 0.54 s.

Simulation

Staying in the level

Camera limits

Source: CSYE 7270 midterm glossary, “Camera” section. Not explained in the chapters.

Level 600 px wide, view 200 px wide, so the view centre is clamped between 120 and 520 px.
