Mesh anatomy

Mesh, vertex, edge and face

Source: Chapter 3, "Apply transforms"; glossary, 3D assets and Blender.

V, E and F are counted in code from the face list. The check V minus E plus F equals 2 holds for any closed mesh without holes.

Computed

Mesh anatomy

Quads, triangles and the polygon count

Source: glossary, 3D assets and Blender (Quad, Triangle versus face versus vertex count); Chapter 3, low-poly prop.

Counts computed from the face list: triangles = sum over faces of (corners minus 2).

Mesh anatomy

Normals and flipped normals

Source: glossary, 3D assets and Blender (Normal, Flipped normals).

A 12-sided cross-section stands in for a mesh. Brightness is the dot product of the normal and the direction to the light.

Source: Chapter 3, export "apply modifiers"; Blender manual, Modeling, Modifiers.
