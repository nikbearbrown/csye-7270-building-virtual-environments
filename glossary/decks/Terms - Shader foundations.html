mix, step, smoothstep, clamp

Source: glossary, Shader foundations; Godot shading language reference (step, smoothstep, clamp).

Each curve is drawn from its formula: smoothstep is t · t · (3 − 2t) with t = clamp((x − e0) / (e1 − e0), 0, 1).
