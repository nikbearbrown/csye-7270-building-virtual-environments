CSYE 7270 · Terminology · Chapter 5

The game design document

Terms for writing a GDD that others can build from and check

Deck 5 of the topic order. 34 terms. Tags: in the book text means a course chapter, mostly Chapter 5, uses and explains it (26 terms); in the glossary means a glossary row only (8 terms). Assignment 4 asks students to create a GDD with: the name of the game, concept, game flow, game elements and assets, main characters, story, gameplay (goal, mechanics, abilities and power-ups, levels and challenges, losing or restarting, player skills), graphics and styling with visual examples, music and sound effects, and a technical description. It is worth 5 points. This deck covers the sixteen-section structure, the IDs, the tags and the failure modes that Chapter 5 uses to check a GDD, which go beyond that list. Assignment 8, the pitch film, is where the short forms (brief and pitch) are used.
