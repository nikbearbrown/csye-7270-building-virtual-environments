What makes it a game

Goal, opposition, decisions, rules

Source: “So You Wanna Make Games”, game design video.

Tennis has all four. Eating cereal has none. A cereal race has a goal, opposition and rules but no decisions, so it is not yet a game. The cereal race is my own example.
