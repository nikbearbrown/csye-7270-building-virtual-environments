# Conducting AI throughout Building Virtual Environments

The course begins with [Conducting AI Through Games and Virtual Worlds](chapters/preface-conducting-ai.md), an unnumbered preface before Module 0. Its central claim is that games make the division between technical production and human direction unusually tangible. Engineering, art, fun and opinion meet in a playable artifact. The student must explain both what the agent built and why the result is worth accepting.

This foundation applies to every module, assignment and future film. It does not add another graded assignment or renumber Modules 0–15.

## The recurring decision record

Keep five short statements: intended experience; delegated work and boundaries; actual evidence; human judgment; next action and what would change the decision. The record belongs in the existing brief, test report or Frictional log rather than requiring a separate administrative document for every prompt. Do not ask an agent to supply observations on behalf of someone who did not play, listen or inspect.

## Engine comparisons and Why Godot

Every module ends with **Doing the same thing in Unity** followed by **Doing the same thing in Unreal Engine**. The endings give similarities, differences, a companion-chapter link and official documentation. The existing three-engine comparison tables remain nearby. Full engine-specific tutorials are optional further reading, not additional required builds.

The course's primary Godot rationale is direct editing of code and text scenes followed by engine checks, without an MCP bridge. Do not turn that into a claim that Godot has no community MCP integrations, that Unity scenes cannot be text, or that Unity, Unreal and Blender universally require MCP. Distinguish source edits, engine scripting and editor bridges.

The preface dates the integration-maturity discussion to October 2026, cites Unity's beta introduction and Epic's Experimental documentation, and attributes the practical rough-edges assessment to Professor Bear. No prediction about next year's maturity is a demonstrated fact. Recheck the named integrations when revising the course.
