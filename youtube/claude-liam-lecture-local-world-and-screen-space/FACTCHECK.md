# Fact-check (Gate F): Local, World and Screen Space

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked by the docs or by a run), CORRECTED (the source or the docs said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).

**Why.** Position math is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart: a pattern stuck to the wrong space looks fine in a still. The source for this film is a third-party course; the film follows its ideas, not its sentences, and every number on screen was measured.

**Found.** 85 rows: 76 PASS, 3 CORRECTED, 6 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Three things were learned by running: the docs' range for FRAGCOORD is wrong for the engine (it counts pixels); the four ways of reading the screen address agree to the last 8-bit level in every setup tried; a world-locked pattern built from texture pixel size silently depends on the sprite's art. Rows 72 onward were added on 2026-10-13 for the on-screen definitions (GLOSSARY.md wording) and the capture routes; they were appended so no earlier row number moved. No row is OPEN: the Inspector's wording (row 23) was settled by the property list and is now confirmed by the real editor frames recorded by script on 2026-10-13 (E1, clean-root pair).

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated; the position checks were repeated on the Compatibility renderer (`run-7-*.log`) with identical results. Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13. Editor frames (B11 only): `P` = `_godot-shaders-series/editor-capture/png/1920x1080` (folder local-world-and-screen-space; native-size twins in `png/3456x1944`), recorded by script in the real 4.7.2 editor on 2026-10-13, labels read from the live UI (`_godot-shaders-series/editor-capture/README.md`). Scripts, logs and frames: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`RESULTS.md` is the index). In the table, T1 to T5 are the cases of `verify3.gd`, V4a to V4g of `verify4.gd`, W1 to W4 of `verify5.gd`, F1, A1, T1 and H1 of `verify6.gd` (T1 there is the texel-size case), N1 to N3 of `verify7.gd`, S5, S6 and R1 of `verify8.gd`, M1 to M3 of `verify9.gd`, the stretch-mode comparison in `verify10.gd`, the Inspector's rows in `verify11.gd`, the capture scenes C01 to C11b in `verify12.gd`, the two capture routes for the four-tile shaders (A, B, B2) in `verify13.gd`.
