# Planning checks: results

**What this is.** The runs behind the numbers in `FACTCHECK.md`, in the order they were done, with the script, the log, the finding and what it means. All on Bear's Mac: Apple M4 Pro, Godot 4.7.2 stable (official), Forward+ unless stated, 2026-10-13.
**Why.** So any figure on screen can be re-run. Failed and inconclusive runs are kept and labelled.
**Found.** Thirteen verify scripts (`verify1.gd` to `verify13.gd`), the reference scene scripts for the twelve Godot captures, one Lab batch and two scratch Lab probes. Two runs did not give an answer first time (two probe pixels missed the sprite; the first Lab batch dropped its arguments) and both are labelled below. Headline findings: the four ways of reading the screen address agree in every setup tried; FRAGCOORD counts pixels although the documentation says its far corner is (1.0, 1.0); a hint-less sampler clamps; the 4K capture route triples screen-pixel numbers and leaves world and local numbers alone.

How to re-run: copy this folder's `*.gd` and `project.godot` into a scratch Godot project (the scripts load each other as `res://probe_lib.gd`, `res://scenes_lib.gd`, `res://sky_lib.gd`; `scenes_lib.gd` holds the absolute path of this film's `lab-shaders/`) and run `godot --path <project> --script res://verifyN.gd` (windowed, not headless; add `--rendering-method gl_compatibility` for Compatibility). The Lab batch is `./run-lab-checks.sh`. The generator for the sky is `make_sky.gd` (headless is fine).

**How the probes work.** `probe_lib.gd` has one encoder shader that writes any chosen number as a 16-bit value into the red and green channels (`floor(v * scale + 1024)`, high byte and low byte, both over 255), and `read()` decodes it from one pixel. Fragment centres sit at +0.5, so `floor()` of an interpolated position is the integer pixel index. That makes positions exact to 1/16 pixel (scale 16) without a floating-point render target.

## Process notes (so nothing here is mistaken for a clean run)

- The first run of `run-lab-checks.sh` passed `--size` and `--kind` in an unquoted zsh variable; zsh does not split it, the Lab silently ignored the options and every render was a 640 by 360 ColorRect. The results were discarded and the script now keeps its arguments in arrays (see its header). That first output was not saved.
- One direct `godot --path <Lab>` invocation was made outside `check_shader.sh` while trying to read the broken shader's raw log (it was killed at once by a mistyped pipe; no output was used and no other run was affected). The raw log was then taken in a scratch project (`broken.gd`).
- The fragment definition was first tried as a comment inside `04-broken-matrix-in-fragment.gdshader` (line 2). The engine prints the shader's source with comments stripped, so the printed window showed a blank line and the idea failed. The shader file was restored to its original text and the two Lab rows for `04-broken` (Forward+ and Compatibility) put back; during the attempt the Lab ran that shader once on each renderer (same compile error both times) and overwrote `png/04-broken.png` and `png-gl_compatibility/04-broken.png` with the same kind of image. The fix that worked is the printed line in `broken.gd` (run 16).
- `verify12.gd` was first run (run 15) with C10 at 1280 by 720 through the window; the series rule changed during the layout (2026-10-13, GLOSSARY.md and COMPONENT-SPECS.md) and C10 was moved to 1920 by 1080 at scale 1, `verify12.gd` patched to build scenes whose `route()` reads window pixels into a SubViewport of their own, and re-run (run 17). The first log is kept under its new name; every other capture gives identical results in both runs.
- `verify5.gd` and `verify6.gd` read frames at 1280 by 720; eleven captures are 3840 by 2160 through the 4K route (C10 is 1920 by 1080), so the on-screen look is sharper than the PNGs here.
