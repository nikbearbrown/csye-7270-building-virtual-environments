# Shot list: Local, World and Screen Space

**What this is.** One row per body beat: what the sentence is about, the picture chosen, the lane that came second, and why this one won. Every beat was auditioned against at least one other lane.

**Why.** The lecture skill's BEST-BEAT LAW: no lane is the default and none has a quota; the picture whose motion is the claim wins. A reviewer can disagree with any row without reading code.

**Found.** 40 body beats. Lane histogram: manim 24, capture 12, remotion 2, editor 1, terminal 1. Every Godot render shown is a Shader Lab, scene-script or editor capture of a tested file in `lab-shaders/`. Six things must be built or recorded first (below).

## Must exist before the build

1. **`ShaderLab`** Remotion component (spec: `../_godot-shaders-series/COMPONENT-SPECS.md`; shared, built once). 2. **`shader_kit.py`** Manim helpers (same file) **plus four film-local helpers** this film needs and the kit does not list: `world_window_scene` (the world rectangle, the window frame and the sprite with its corner dot, used by B03 to B07 and B26 to B34), `origin_marker` (a ring with a right arrow and a down arrow), `address_tag` (a number pair that attaches to a point and can count), `matrix_table` (a 4 by 4 grid of outline cells with one column or row highlightable). 3. **Capture harness** `lab/capture.gd` + `capture_shader.sh` with the scene-script route (same file); the reference scene scripts are `evidence/planning-checks/capture_scenes.gd` (contract `build` / `at(t)` / `route()`, one wrapper per capture in `capture-scenes/`), built on `scenes_lib.gd` and `sky_lib.gd`; `route()` says whether a capture goes through the 4K SubViewport route or, for shaders that read window pixels (C10 only), 1920 by 1080 at scale 1 (the series rule). 4. **Editor frame E1** (B11) is already recorded by script (copy it; E2 is optional for B14) and the **docs screenshot D2** (B31) is taken with Playwright (see `CAPTURE-PLAN.md`). 5. **The sky texture** (procedural, seed 7: `sky_lib.gd`) and the **checker** (B23). 6. **The broken-shader log** for B14 (`broken.gd`, re-run at build time).
