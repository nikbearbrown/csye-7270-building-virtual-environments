# Fact-check (Gate F): UV Space

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the source said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition or an analogy, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** An address that "moves the picture the other way" and a circle that "turns oval for a reason" are exactly the claims a confident source gets wrong. The source for this film is a long third-party course, loose in places; nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently, and every number on screen was measured.
**Found.** 69 rows: 61 PASS, 3 CORRECTED, 5 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Three things were learned by running that the sources do not say: a texture's own repeat setting applies to a shader's reads on a `TextureRect` although the docs say it does not (row 29); `TEXTURE_PIXEL_SIZE` is one by one on a `ColorRect` and `SCREEN_PIXEL_SIZE` is the screen's, so only a uniform is right on every node (rows 51 to 53); and sweeping a flip through zero shows one stretched row, so the film flips in one step (row 42). No row is OPEN: the title is spoken "U V Space" by the series' spoken-form rule (row 67), and the build's voice check listens for it. The lookup is also pixel-identical to no shader at all (row 11).

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated (the geometry rows were also run on Compatibility and are identical). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13: `tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/canvas_item_shader.html`, `.../shading_language.html`, `.../shader_functions.html`, `tutorials/shaders/introduction_to_shaders.html`, `tutorials/shaders/your_first_shader/your_first_2d_shader.html`, `classes/class_canvasitem.html`. Logs and scripts: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`run-lab-checks.sh` with `lab-results.jsonl`, `lab-summary.txt` and `png/`; `measure.py`; `verify-aspect.gd`, `verify-repeat.gd`, `verify-scroll.gd`, `verify-identity.gd` with `run-1-aspect.log`, `run-2-repeat.log`, `run-3-scroll.log`, `run-4-identity.log`).
