# Fact-check (Gate F): Reading the Screen

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked against the docs or by a run), CORRECTED (the source, the docs' wording or the brief said something looser or different and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition or a design judgement, labelled as one), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart, and the sources (a long course and a short intro, both third party) are loose in places. The one rule that decides the last act, how many copies of the screen exist, is stated in the docs without the layer, so it was run rather than trusted.
**Found.** 77 rows: 60 PASS, 7 CORRECTED, 10 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Three findings changed the film: a layer takes its own copy (the plan had a stale-copy trap that does not exist across layers); plain `position / size` is wrong once a camera moves; and `repeat_enable`, the default filter and `filter_nearest` behave differently in Forward+ and Compatibility, so the film claims none of them for both (it names the renderer that obeys `filter_nearest`, once, in B36). One finding is about the recording, not the engine: a mip-level blur is less than half as strong when captured through the 3840 x 2160 SubViewport route, so that one capture (C13) is recorded natively at 1920 x 1080. Every editor label the film names was read from the real editor (a plugin walked the Inspector and its menus in Godot 4.7.2), which also closes the Inspector rows the first film of the series left open.

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated; every run was also made in the Compatibility renderer and differences are named. Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13. Editor frames (pointers only; this film records none): `P` = `_godot-shaders-series/editor-capture/png/1920x1080` (recorded by script in the real 4.7.2 editor on 2026-10-13 for other films; native twins in `png/3456x1944`). Logs and scripts: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`verify1.gd` to `verify10.gd`, `scene_c01` to `scene_c23`, `run-lab-checks.sh`, `lab-results.jsonl`, `editor-labels/`, `run-NN-*.log`; the index is `RESULTS.md`).
