# Fact-check (Gate F): Vertex Shaders

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (a source said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, a framing line or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A vertex shader is where "it compiles" and "it looks right" are furthest apart: the commonest failures (a flag that does not wave, a mesh that draws upside down, a position in the wrong space) all compile. The brief's source is a third-party course; nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently.
**Found.** 76 rows: 58 PASS, 1 CORRECTED, 17 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Everything the film asserts about Godot was either read in the 4.7 documentation (quoted below) or run in Godot 4.7.2. One docs sentence is worded for 3D and corrected for 2D (row 28). Three runs did not go cleanly and are kept: the vertex-id check saturated for the quad mesh in its first version (verify4, then fixed), the first TIME-based frames were taken with the window covered and TIME froze (flag_motion.py, re-taken with the window kept on top), and an every-frame reading loop also froze TIME (see RESULTS.md).

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless a row says Compatibility. Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13: `tutorials/shaders/introduction_to_shaders.html`, `tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/canvas_item_shader.html`, `tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/shading_language.html`, `classes/class_polygon2d.html`, `classes/class_meshinstance2d.html`, `classes/class_quadmesh.html`, `classes/class_planemesh.html`, `classes/class_mesh.html`, `classes/class_shadermaterial.html`, `classes/class_geometry2d.html`.
