# Fact-check (Gate F): Soft Edges, Glow and Noise

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the source said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, an analogy or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart, and the source for this film (a third-party video build of an energy shield) gets smoothstep's arithmetic wrong and leaves out what glow needs. Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently.
**Found.** 98 rows: 78 PASS, 4 CORRECTED, 16 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Seven things were learned by running, not by reading: swapped or equal `smoothstep` edges "work" on this Mac although the docs call them undefined; a power of a negative number is NaN for a fractional exponent here; `hint_range` does not clamp what code sends; adding to a lookup moves the picture the other way, by exactly the amount added; a plain `NoiseTexture2D` has a wrap seam fourteen times a normal step and `seamless` removes it; glow in 2D needs the environment's **background mode Canvas** (a WorldEnvironment alone changes nothing) and HDR 2D to carry real color; and turning HDR 2D on washes out any color uniform that lacks `source_color`. The two rows that were OPEN (35, 57), the Inspector's on-screen widgets, were closed on 2026-10-13 by real editor frames recorded by script (E3: this film's own bubble material; E1: two frames recorded for the Textures and Color film, which show the same noise views): both PASS, and no word of the film changed.

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated (Compatibility rows are labelled). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13; the pages were downloaded as HTML and the quotes below are verbatim from the text. Editor frames: `P` = `_godot-shaders-series/editor-capture/png/1920x1080` (folders soft-edges-glow-and-noise, textures-and-color, godot-shaders; native-size twins in `png/3456x1944` except for this film's own two frames), recorded by script in the real 4.7.2 editor on 2026-10-13, labels read from the live UI (`_godot-shaders-series/editor-capture/README.md`). Logs and scripts: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`run-lab-checks.sh` + `lab-analysis.txt`, `run-glow-checks.sh` + `glow-summary.txt`, `noise_check.gd` + `run-noise.log`, `extra_checks.gd` + `run-extra.log`, `toggle_check.gd` + `run-toggle.log`, `finale_check.gd` + `run-finale.log`).
