# Shot list: Godot Shader Language

**What this is.** One row per body beat: what the sentence is about, the picture chosen, the lane that came second, and why this one won. Every beat was auditioned against at least one other lane.

**Why.** The lecture skill's BEST-BEAT LAW: no lane is the default and none has a quota; the picture whose motion is the claim wins. A reviewer can disagree with any row without reading code.

**Found.** 41 body beats. Lane histogram: manim 22, capture 8, remotion 6, terminal 5. Every Godot render shown is a Shader Lab capture of a tested file in `lab-shaders/`; every error shown is the engine's own log, word for word, from `evidence/planning-checks/logs/`. Seven things must be built or recorded first (below).

## Must exist before the build

1. **`ShaderLab`** Remotion component (spec: `../_godot-shaders-series/COMPONENT-SPECS.md`; reused if the first shader film's build already made it). 2. **`shader_kit.py`** Manim helpers (same file). 3. **Capture harness** `lab/capture.gd` + `capture_shader.sh` (same file; this film needs `--set`, `--time-origin`, a still PNG mode and two live-TIME clips of about 16 s and 12 s, nothing scripted). 4. **`lab_window(png, width, caption)`** in the film's `scenes.py` (or `shader_kit.py`): an outlined frame holding a real Lab PNG with a one-line caption, used by B07, B08, B29 and B32. 5. **Type-tag helper** in the film's `scenes.py`: a small outlined tag placed under a span of a code line with a bracket (B19, B35 to B39). 6. **Docs screenshots D1 and D2** via Playwright (BrowserCapture convention). 7. **Terminal logs**: no capture needed; the lanes already carry the engine's lines verbatim from `evidence/planning-checks/logs/`.
