# Course-owned scene design

The scene library was searched before authoring. Canonical bookends are imported without changing the shared registry. A reel-local entry point prevents interference with other active film builds.

- `CourseScene`: scene tree, ownership list, three-part specification, goal/deferred-scope separation, level-extension schematic, art-readability study, semester timeline and delivery ledger. Reusable within this reel; not promoted to the shared library without review.
- `CourseEvidence`: full-frame fit of genuine archived game stills, with source/limitation label. The image retains every source pixel in its aspect ratio; there is no fabricated Godot interface.
- Twelve `CourseMechanism` scenes: production dependencies and cycles, supervisory-capacity map, collider/art separation, controlled revision and responsibility boundary. All drawn natively at 3840 × 2160.

Archive extracts are also native 3840 × 2160 according to their original capture receipts. They were generated by Godot with synthetic keyboard input, not human play. Still-image use is intentional evidence inspection; it must not be mistaken for a new playtest.

Typography: EB Garamond, Inter, PT Mono. Ink on warm cream. Terracotta indicates active structure, not small text. No background music during narration, no stock b-roll, no generated raster filler, no burned-in subtitles.

Render route: `scripts/lock-and-render.py` adapts the canonical `remotion_scenes.py` renderer to the local entry point; Manim scenes render at 4K/24 fps. `compile.py` assembles the measured clock. SRT comes from actual word alignment, not truncated beat paragraphs.
