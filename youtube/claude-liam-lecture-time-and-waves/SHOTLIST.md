# Shot list: Time and Waves

**What this is.** One row per body beat: what the sentence is about, the picture chosen, the lane that came second, and why this one won. Every beat was auditioned against at least one other lane.

**Why.** The lecture skill's BEST-BEAT LAW: no lane is the default and none has a quota; the picture whose motion is the claim wins. A reviewer can disagree with any row without reading code.

**Found.** 40 body beats. Lane histogram: capture 18, manim 18, remotion 4. Every Godot render shown is a Shader Lab render or a scene capture of a tested file in `lab-shaders/`; no editor frame is needed. Six things must exist before the build (below).

## Must exist before the build

1. **`ShaderLab`** Remotion component with `layout` `side`, `video` and `code` (spec: `../_godot-shaders-series/COMPONENT-SPECS.md`; B17 uses the `code` layout). 2. **`shader_kit.py`** Manim helpers (`plot`, `ramp_strip`, `ramp_marker`, `pixel_grid`; same file). 3. **Capture harness** `lab/capture.gd` + `capture_shader.sh`, plus support for the eight **scene scripts** (ten captures) in `evidence/planning-checks/scene-project/scenes/` (a scene root script, a frame counter, a scripted click, `Engine.time_scale`, `paused`, a CurveTexture made in code; every one already runs under Movie Maker at 30 fps: `scene-project/logs/`). 4. **Film-local Manim helpers** (not in the shared kit; promote them if another film wants them): `knob_slider(label, lo, hi)` for B14 to B16 and B30, `time_ruler(label, zero_offset)` for B05 and B06, `curve_strip(curve)` for B22 and B24, `bracket(a, b, label)`. 5. **Docs screenshot D1** for B02 via Playwright. 6. **No editor frames**: nothing in this film shows the Inspector (B22 to B24 show the CurveTexture as an idea and a script-made resource).
