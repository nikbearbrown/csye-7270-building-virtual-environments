# Fact-check (Gate F): Time and Waves

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked by the docs, by a run, or by arithmetic), CORRECTED (the source or the brief said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition or advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader is where "it compiles" and "it looks right" are furthest apart, and the film's source is a third-party course that repeats an error about `TIME`. Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently: the numbers come from runs on Bear's Mac.
**Found.** 85 rows: 78 PASS, 3 CORRECTED, 4 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Three things were learned by running, not by reading: the tick counter and `TIME` have different zeros and drift apart under a time scale (rows B05, B06); the wrap jumps only the pulses that do not fit it (row B04); a tween on a uniform fails unless the uniform was set from code first (row B40). One side finding is kept out of the film: `TIME` only advances while the window is being drawn (last row). Nothing is OPEN.

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated (the probe checks also ran in Compatibility: `twin-compat-summary.txt`, `image-compat-summary.txt`). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13; the passages are in `evidence/planning-checks/docs-quotes.md`. Scripts and logs: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`gd/`, `scene-project/`, `run_twins.py`, `run_images.py`, `run-lab-compile.sh`). Frame-by-frame checks of the ten scene captures: `scene-project/logs/decode-scenes-*.txt`. Real-time checks vary a little from run to run (frame timing, the machine's other work): the numbers quoted are from the logs stored in `gd/logs/`; a full re-run with `run-all.sh` on 2026-10-13 (logs in `evidence/planning-checks/rerun-logs/`) reproduced every finding, and its Movie Maker decodes are identical to the stored ones.
