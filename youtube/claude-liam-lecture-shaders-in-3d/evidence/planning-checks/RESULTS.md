# Planning checks: results

**What this is.** The runs behind the numbers in `FACTCHECK.md`, in the order they were done, with the command, the result and what it means. All on Bear's Mac: Apple M4 Pro, Godot 4.7.2 stable (official), Forward+ unless stated, 2026-10-13.
**Why.** So any figure on screen can be re-run, and so the runs that did not give the answer first time stay visible.
**Found.** One Lab batch (50 records on 19 shader files) and a small extra batch (3 records), fifteen GDScript scripts and three analysis scripts, one labelled failure (a tween on a never-set uniform, which the film warns about) and one superseded attempt (a full turn that loses the flat square). Eight facts came from running rather than reading: the box's six UV cells, six stripes against sixteen, an empty `light()` silencing the lamp, a back face not drawn, a 2D shader ignored on a mesh with no error, a tween on a never-set uniform failing, Forward+ drawing 0.5 as 0.74, and Compatibility's endpoints reading a few steps off the typed numbers. Three more runs back on-screen definitions and one claim: alpha at its ends, `light()` and a lamp out of range, and `COLOR` read in a spatial shader. One analysis found that the pad's stripes are hard to see at half charge, so the held stills are at 0.3.

How the logs were made: `run-script.sh <script> <log>` copies `project.godot` and the `verify*.gd` scripts into a scratch Godot project under `/private/tmp/`, runs `godot --path <scratch> --script res://<script>` in a real window, and writes the log with a header (date, machine, command). The Lab batch is `./run-lab-checks.sh` (writes `lab-results.jsonl` and `png/`); `summarize-lab.py` turns it into `lab-summary.txt`. A second, small batch, `./run-lab-checks-extra.sh`, writes `lab-results-extra.jsonl` (the pad at activation 0.3 in three renderers); it is separate so that each batch file holds exactly the rows of its own script. The Lab serializes runs with a lock shared by every shader film, so a batch can wait for another film's run.

**Side findings for the series (not in the film).**

- The Lab's `quad3d` scene cannot show lit versus unshaded (white ambient light at energy 1, no lamp). A lamp scene is needed, as S3.
- A 2D `COLOR` of 0.5 draws 0.502 in all three renderers; a 3D unshaded `ALBEDO` of 0.5 draws 0.737 in Forward+ and Mobile. Any film that compares a 2D result with a 3D one must say which renderer.
- Mobile reads within one 8-bit step of Forward+ for the pad (`lab-summary.txt`).
- A `BoxMesh` has 24 vertices, not 8: `vertex()` runs 24 times for a box.
- The first `light()` finding: ambient light still reaches a surface whose `light()` is empty, which is why it is dim and not black.

**Batch integrity (checked 2026-10-13 after another session ran `pkill -f run-lab-checks.sh` at about 14:49).** `run-lab-checks.sh` has 50 `run` lines once its three loops are expanded; `lab-results.jsonl` has 50 rows carrying exactly those 50 labels (none missing, none extra, finished 14:53, after the kill); 48 rows exit 0 with a `RESULT` line and have a PNG in `png/`; rows 04 and 05 exit 1 with the compiler's `SHADER ERROR`, on purpose; the batch log ends with its `wrote ...` line; every `run-*.log` ends with an `exit` line. Nothing was re-run. The extra batch has its own 3 `run` lines and 3 rows, all exit 0 with a `RESULT` line and a PNG.

**A note on words.** The scripts and logs here call one stripe of the pad a "band" (`analyze-bands.py`, `verify2-...gd`, their output). They are kept as they were run. In the film and its paperwork the word is "stripe", because the series GLOSSARY now gives "band" another meaning (the strip of pixels near a line).
