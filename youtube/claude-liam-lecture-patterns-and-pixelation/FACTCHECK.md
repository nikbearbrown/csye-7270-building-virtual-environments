# Fact-check (Gate F): Patterns and Pixelation

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the source said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, an analogy or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart, and the one third-party source for this film is loose in places (its pixelate half-fix is the clearest case). Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently.
**Found.** 70 rows: 66 PASS, 1 CORRECTED, 3 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Four things were learned by running, not by reading: the half inside the `floor` removes the drift but halves the edge blocks; the drift is half a block (3.5 picture pixels at 8-pixel blocks); percent on decimals is a shader error while `mod` and `fract` stay in range for negative numbers; and the "Your turn" prompt has an answer that passes both of its checks while each plausible wrong answer fails one. One run was inconclusive and is kept (row 62).

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated (identical pixels on Compatibility where checked, row 65). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13. Logs and scripts: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`run-lab-checks.sh`, `lab-results.jsonl`, `verify-patterns.py`, `analysis-summary.txt`, `verify-bhtf.gd`, `png/`, `reference-shaders/`). Docs addresses: `.../shader_reference/shader_functions.html` (functions), `.../shader_reference/canvas_item_shader.html` (built-ins), `.../shader_reference/shading_language.html` (types and uniforms), `.../introduction_to_shaders.html`.
