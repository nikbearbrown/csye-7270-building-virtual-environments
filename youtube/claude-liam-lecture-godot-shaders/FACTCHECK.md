# Fact-check (Gate F): Godot Shaders

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the source said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, an analogy or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart, and the sources for this film (a short intro video and a seven-hour course, both third-party) are loose in places. Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently.
**Found.** 58 rows: 47 PASS, 5 CORRECTED, 4 EXEMPT, 2 OPEN. Four things were learned by running, not by reading: `TIME` is one global clock; a tween on a uniform fails unless the uniform has been set from code first; `light()` runs once per light and the results add; and uniform names are case-sensitive. One run was inconclusive and is kept (row 46). Two OPEN rows (38, 39) are the Inspector's wording and widgets, confirmed when editor frames E2 and E3 are captured.

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated. Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13. Logs and scripts: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`verify.gd`, `verify2.gd`, `verify3.gd`, `verify4.gd`, `verify5.gd`, `verify6.gd`, `run-lab-checks.sh`, `lab-results.jsonl`, `lean-edge.txt`).
