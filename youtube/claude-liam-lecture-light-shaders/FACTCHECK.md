# Fact-check (Gate F): Light Shaders

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the source or the docs said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, an analogy or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** Lighting is where "it runs" and "it looks right" are furthest apart, and the long third-party course this film follows says one thing that is wrong for Godot 4.7 (that a light function cannot read U V). Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently: every Godot behaviour was either read in the 4.7 documentation or measured in a real Godot window.
**Found.** 83 rows: 69 PASS, 3 CORRECTED, 11 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. The rebuilt default matches Godot's default in every pixel (row 28) but not when a normal map is present (row 39); the docs' wording of LIGHT_DIRECTION is loose and was measured (row 42); screen-space numbers are physical pixels, which changes how the film's captures must be recorded (row 75). Failed and inconclusive runs are kept in `evidence/planning-checks/` (parse error, probe bugs, a hung script, a coarse 8-bit decode) and named in `RESULTS.md`.

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated; every check was repeated on the Compatibility renderer and agreed within one step of 255 (logs named `*-compat.log`). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13. Scripts and logs: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`verify1.gd` to `verify9.gd`, `light_scene.gd`, `captures.json`, `lab-results.jsonl`).
