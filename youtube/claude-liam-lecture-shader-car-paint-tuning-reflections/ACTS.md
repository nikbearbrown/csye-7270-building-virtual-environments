# Car Paint: Tuning Reflections

Learning objective: Separate reflection controls from the two specular highlight lobes.

Opening: portrait/title, corrected misconception, three prerequisites.

Act — Trace The Model: B01–B04 introduce the inputs, coordinate/data contract and central calculation.

Act — Test The Model: B05–B08 demonstrate a controlled change, examine the implementation and state its limitation.

Closing: four-point recap, predictive exercise with two checks, Liam sign-off and exact-title outro.

Cast: the same sphere, torus, camera and generated textures throughout.

Coverage: all executable source stages are assigned to the two acts. Existing lighting terms are summarized as prerequisites where they are not the target mechanism. LEFT OUT: copied-source historical comments, host metadata, unused terms, and unrelated engine features. No claim is made that this is a physically complete material.
