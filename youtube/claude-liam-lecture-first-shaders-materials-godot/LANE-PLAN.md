# LANE-PLAN.md — suggested pictures, per beat

SUGGESTIONS, not orders. The BEST-BEAT LAW decides: audition at least two lanes per beat, pick the one whose MOTION is the beat's claim, write the pick and the runner-up in your lanes file. Where a real capture, log or file exists it wins over a drawing of it (SHOW-THE-THING LAW). Evidence names are in `EVIDENCE-INDEX.md`; numbers on screen come from the logs, never from memory. The narration (`narration.py`, `beat_sheet.json`) is fixed: do not edit it; report problems.

HONESTY CHIPS (use `example_tag()` / `status_tag()` from the kit): every beat that shows a file, log or capture of the INSTRUCTOR EXAMPLE carries `example_tag()` ("instructor example · A. I.-written") at some point; every prompt of the assignment is shown with `status_tag("documented, not run")`; scripted-input frames carry `status_tag("scripted input")`; frames from the re-run carry `status_tag("re-run for this film")`; drawings of an idea carry nothing but are never styled as a screenshot. A tool is shown as itself: Godot by its real window frames (editor frames, engine captures), Claude Code by the library's skin (nothing typed that was not typed), GitHub/Poly Haven by their logo (`logo_chip`) only where a company is named.

Builder assignment (scene files `scenes_act<N>.py`, N = the act number below; the preflight takes N):
