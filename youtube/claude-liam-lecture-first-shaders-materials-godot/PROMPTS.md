# PROMPTS.md — generation prompts

No generation prompts. Nothing in this film was made by a paid or AI image/video generation service; every picture is a real engine capture or editor frame, a real log, a crop of a real texture, or a Manim/Remotion drawing. Narration is Kokoro (free, local). The prompts the film SHOWS (the assignment's context-file, driver and audit prompts and its `claude -p` commands) are the assignment's own text, labelled "documented, not run"; they were not sent to any model for this film.
