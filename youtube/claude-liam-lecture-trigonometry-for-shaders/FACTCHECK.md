# Fact-check (Gate F): Trigonometry for Shaders

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (a source or an earlier draft said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, an analogy or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** Trigonometry in a shader is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart: the wrong angle unit, the wrong direction and the wrong arc tangent all compile. Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently, and every prediction was computed from the maths before the Godot reading was compared with it.
**Found.** 66 rows: 60 PASS, 1 CORRECTED, 5 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Five things were learned by running, not by reading: `PI`, `TAU` and `E` are documented only in each shader type's reference (not in the shading-language page) and compile undeclared; skipping the radians conversion compiles silently and `sin(30.0)` is about -0.99; the moon at 30 degrees lands BELOW the middle; the one-argument `atan(y / x)` gives opposite directions the same answer; and on a wide shape circles come out oval (160 by 90 pixels) until x is scaled by width over height. No run failed; the first Lab runner was stopped while it queued (see `evidence/planning-checks/RESULTS.md`).

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS 26.5.1, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated (plain-colour shaders were also read in Compatibility and match). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13; exact quotes in `evidence/planning-checks/docs-quotes.txt`. Scripts and logs: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`verify-math.gd` and `run-1-verify-math.log`; `run_lab_checks.py`, `lab-results.jsonl`, `lab-summary.txt`; `run_batch.py`, `batch-summary.txt`; `png/`). "Lab NN" names a run in `lab-summary.txt`; "batch X" names a case in `batch-summary.txt`; "math X" names a check in `run-1-verify-math.log`.
