# Fact-check (Gate F): Drawing Shapes with Math

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the source said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, an opinion or a labelled demonstration), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart, and the one third-party source for this film (a video course, known only through the content brief) is loose in two places. Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently: every picture was rendered in Godot 4.7.2 and compared, pixel by pixel, with an independent calculation, and every number said or shown was computed by Godot itself.
**Found.** 84 rows: 68 PASS, 2 CORRECTED, 14 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Two things were learned by running, not by reading: an alpha above one or below zero looks exactly like one or zero on screen in a default project but still changes the next sum (so the cutout vanishes, and a spot cut off by a bite never appears), and `UV` and `COLOR` are not visible inside your own functions while `TIME` is. Nothing is left OPEN: the one claim that needed a real editor window (row 69, six sliders in the Inspector) was observed in a planning-check frame; capture E1 re-records it cleanly for the film.

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS 26.5.1, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ (Metal) unless stated; the hard-edged shapes were also rendered on Compatibility and agree pixel for pixel (row 72). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13. Logs, scripts and pictures: `evidence/planning-checks/` (`run-lab-checks.sh`, `lab-results.jsonl`, `calc-results.txt`, `analysis-masks.txt`, `analysis-calcs.txt`, `analysis-crosshair-and-renderers.txt`, `analysis-your-turn.txt`, `verify-a.gd`, `verify-b.gd`, `verify-c.gd` and their `run-*.log`). "The model" means `analyze-masks.py`: an independent float64 calculation of the same maths; "0 of 230,400 pixels differ" means every pixel of a 480 by 480 render matches it.
