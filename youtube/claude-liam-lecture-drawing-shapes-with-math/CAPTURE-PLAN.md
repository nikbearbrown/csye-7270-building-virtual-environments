Every shader compiles (72 Lab runs, `evidence/planning-checks/lab-results.jsonl`); every probe value below was read in a real Godot 4.7.2 window on Forward+ on an Apple M4 Pro; every render matches an independent float64 model on every one of its 230,400 pixels (`analysis-masks.txt`, `analysis-crosshair-and-renderers.txt`), and the hard-edged ones agree with the Compatibility renderer pixel for pixel. Everything is a SQUARE result window. ## Conventions for every capture

- **Square.** Logical canvas 1080 by 1080, rendered at 2160 by 2160 on the SubViewport native-size route (`../_godot-shaders-series/COMPONENT-SPECS.md`, "Capture harness"); never upscale a small render. Every shape is written in UV units, so the picture is the same at any square size; the probes below were read in the Lab's 480 by 480 view, which is the only size this plan has run.
- **30 fps, offline fixed tick rate.** Nothing here says anything about real-time frame rate; never claim one. A Godot render is not a recording of a game running.
- **Renderer: Forward+** for every capture (Godot's default for a new project), **default project settings** (2D HDR off: with it on, an alpha above 1 or below 0 is not clamped and B14's claim would not hold; `run-d-hdr2d-alpha-clamp.log`). The hard-edged shapes were also rendered on Compatibility and agree pixel for pixel; the soft `01-distance` picture differs by at most one level of 255 (`analysis-crosshair-and-renderers.txt`).
- **Stage color** `#D9D5CA` (the Lab's); the shapes are teal (0.37, 0.64, 0.57) written in the shaders; outside a mask the stage shows through.
- **Scripted input is labelled.** Every sweep, hold and jump below is moved by a script, and the `videoLabel` says so. Nothing here uses a real mouse or key.
- **Timelines are fractions of the clip, and the clip is the measured audio of its beat.** Pass `--length SECONDS` (the harness spec allows fractional keyframes), so record after the audio is generated (BUILD-PROMPT: audio first). The "about N s" figures are estimates for orientation only. The breakpoints below were placed by `_gen_lanes.py` from where the words are estimated to fall; **after the audio and `align.py`, run `python3 _gen_lanes.py --times`: it prints the same breakpoints re-derived from the measured words, in the harness's own `--animate` and `--steps` syntax**, with the holds built in. Use those; C01's are fixed.
- **Holds at the sample marks.** The gates sample the 50 % and 85 % frames, so every video below is timed to have its 50 % and 85 % frames inside a hold (or, for the stepped C24, a settled state): no move starts or ends between 44 % and 56 %, and the last move ends by 82 %. Nothing on the ShaderLab layers (cues, readouts, rings) starts between 45 % and 55 % of the beat.
- **Each dial runs 0 then 1 and ends on 1** in the sweep videos (C21 to C23), because the voice says "grows", "thickens" and "pushes outward": it drops from its default to 0, holds, then rises to 1. **The first dial of a clip then stays at 1.0 while the second moves**, so the 50 % frame shows (first at 1, second at 0) and the 85 % frame (both at 1); both of those pairs were rendered in the Lab (`pair-*` runs; coverage figures in the table), as were all single-dial extremes.
- **Coverage figures are for the 480 by 480 Lab view.** Probe colours are size-independent and must match exactly in the film's capture; coverage moves in the third decimal at 2160 by 2160 (the independent model computed at 2160: C13 0.0421, C14 0.0301, C15 0.0602, where the 480 figures are 0.0408, 0.0292, 0.0583; the disc pictures C09 and C10 move by 0.0001), so compare coverage within 0.003.
- **Output** to `brutalist.art/runtime/remotion/public/godot-shaders/drawing-shapes/` (this film's own folder, because other films also use `godot-shaders/`), with the file names the lanes use.

## Editor frames (Godot's own editor, never drawn from memory)

Method (proven 2026-10-09): `godot --editor --path <scratch copy> <scene> --write-movie out/f.png --fixed-fps 30 --quit-after N`, take a frame. Always a scratch copy. **The driver for this film's view is proven (2026-10-13) and saved:** `evidence/planning-checks/editor-driver/` (a ten-line `EditorPlugin` that switches to 2D and calls `EditorInterface.edit_resource(material)`, plus the project and scene files and a README). Rules from running it: write the frames OUTSIDE the project folder (frames inside `res://` are imported as assets and slow the editor to 13 % of real time); run `godot --headless --path <project> --import` first. **Recommended, not run:** record at 3840 by 2160 with the editor's display scale at 200 % so the cropped Inspector is sharp (the planning check ran at 1920 by 1080 and read the six sliders fine). If the driver does not work for the three states, **Bear records the clips** (one script for the whole series is gathered in `RECORDING-SESSION.md`).
