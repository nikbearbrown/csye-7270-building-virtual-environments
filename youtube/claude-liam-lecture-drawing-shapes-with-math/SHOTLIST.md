# Shot list: Drawing Shapes with Math

**What this is.** One row per body beat: what the sentence is about, the picture chosen, the lane that came second, and why this one won. Every beat was auditioned against at least one other lane.

**Why.** The lecture skill's BEST-BEAT LAW: no lane is the default and none has a quota; the picture whose motion is the claim wins. A reviewer can disagree with any row without reading code.

**Found.** 44 body beats. Lane histogram: capture 25, manim 15, remotion 3, editor 1. Every Godot render shown is a Shader Lab or editor capture of a tested file in `lab-shaders/`; every number on screen was computed or measured in Godot 4.7.2 (`evidence/planning-checks/`). The capture lane is the largest because this film is about code and its picture; each of those beats was weighed against a drawing and won on evidence, not on habit. Every cue time in `lanes/` is derived from where the words fall in the narration (`_gen_lanes.py`), and none starts inside the 45 to 55 % window the gates sample.

## Must exist before the build

1. **`ShaderLab`** Remotion component (spec: `../_godot-shaders-series/COMPONENT-SPECS.md`), with a **square result window** (every result in this film is 1:1; the spec shows only 16:9). 2. **`shader_kit.py`** Manim objects used here: `ramp_strip`, `pixel_grid`, `plot`, `uv_square`; this film adds film-local helpers in `scenes.py` (a disc, a slider with a knob, a wall-and-box, a two-circle Venn). 3. **Capture harness** `lab/capture.gd` + `capture_shader.sh` (piecewise `--animate`, `--steps`, `--set-at`, `--scene` and fractional keyframes with `--length` are all in the spec), which must also give square output. 4. **Editor frames E1** (see `CAPTURE-PLAN.md`; E2, an editor view of the compile error, is an optional upgrade for B32). 5. Registered patterns used: `LectureEvidenceTable`, `LectureCodeTrace` (checked with `./brutalist.art/art scenes --check`).
