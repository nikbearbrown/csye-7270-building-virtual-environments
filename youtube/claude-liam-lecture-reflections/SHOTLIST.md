# Shot list: Reflections

**What this is.** One row per body beat: what the sentence is about, the picture chosen, the lane that came second, and why this one won. Every beat was auditioned against at least one other lane.

**Why.** The lecture skill's BEST-BEAT LAW: no lane is the default and none has a quota; the picture whose motion is the claim wins. A reviewer can disagree with any row without reading code.

**Found.** 37 body beats. Lane histogram: capture 19, manim 12, remotion 4, editor 2. Every Godot render shown is a capture of a scene in `evidence/planning-checks/capture_scenes.gd` (nineteen scripted scenes, each checked by `scene_runner.gd`) or a real editor frame of a scratch project; every shader shown is a tested file in `lab-shaders/`. Seven things must be built or recorded first (below).

## Must exist before the build

1. **`ShaderLab`** Remotion component (spec: `../_godot-shaders-series/COMPONENT-SPECS.md`). 2. **`shader_kit.py`** Manim helpers (same file), **plus the objects this film adds**: the pond picture (flat shapes), the slab stack with a thumbnail and a copy frame, node chips, isometric sheets, a ramp strip zoomed to ticks, a colour plane with two circles. 3. **Capture harness** `lab/capture.gd` + `capture_shader.sh` (same file); it must load `evidence/planning-checks/capture_scenes.gd` (contract at the top of that file; `scene_runner.gd` is the checked stand-in). Every capture in a FRESH Godot process. 4. **Editor frames E1 and E2** from the scratch pond project (`make-editor-project.sh`). 5. **Docs screenshots D1 and D2** via Playwright. 6. `BrowserCapture`, `LectureEvidenceTable`, `LectureCodeTrace` exist and are registered. 7. The five tested shader files in `lab-shaders/`.
