# Fact-check (Gate F): Reflections

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the brief or the source said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, an opinion or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader film is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart, and the order in which things are drawn is the kind of fact that is easy to get backwards. The source for this film is a third-party video course; nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently.
**Found.** 71 rows: 60 PASS, 3 CORRECTED, 8 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Everything the film shows was run or read. Four things were learned by running, not by reading: a Rect-mode BackBufferCopy copies only a corner of the screen whatever the node's position, and the box counts render pixels (rows 30, 32 and 71); a copy only serves shaders on its own canvas layer (row 26); equals against 128 over 255 passes in one renderer and fails in the other (row 55); and the pure-versus-half-strength map values read back differently with HDR 2D on (row 58). One run was inconclusive and is kept (run-02, rect mode in a process that already had history).

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated; every pixel result was repeated in the Compatibility renderer (logs with the suffix -compat, identical unless a row says otherwise). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13. Logs and scripts: `evidence/planning-checks/` (index: `RESULTS.md`; "run-NN" below means `run-NN-*.log`).
