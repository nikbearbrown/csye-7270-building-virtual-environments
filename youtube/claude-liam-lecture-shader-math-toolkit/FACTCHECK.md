# Fact-check (Gate F): The Shader Math Toolkit

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the third-party source said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, a teaching frame or design advice, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader is where "it runs" and "it is right" are furthest apart, and the source this film follows (a long third-party course on 2D shaders) is wrong or loose in three places that matter here (`smoothstep` drawn as a straight line, "swap the arguments to invert", the reason a circle turns oval) and silent on a fourth (`mod` of a negative number). Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently.
**Found.** 75 rows: 63 PASS, 3 CORRECTED, 9 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Every function the film names was probed in a real Godot 4.7.2 window at exact inputs (`quarter-tables.md`, `function-tables.md`); every curve the film draws was compared pixel for pixel with Godot's own render (`dense-compare.md`: 21 curves of 640 pixels, 10 round pictures of 129,600 pixels, largest difference one 8-bit level). The film's one new number, `smoothstep(0, 10, 1)` = 0.028 (not 0.1), is measured (0.02797). Two things Godot's docs call "undefined" (`smoothstep` with reversed edges, `pow` of a negative base) were run to see what this Mac does; the film does not rely on either (rows 24 and 61).

Machine: Apple M4 Pro (Apple9), macOS 26.5.1, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ on Metal 4.0 (`evidence/planning-checks/run-2-verify-env.log`). Docs: Godot 4.7 (`docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/` and the pinned `/en/4.7/` address, same text), read 2026-10-13. Logs and scripts are in `evidence/planning-checks/` (index: `RESULTS.md`). 8-bit note: a pixel read from a render is a whole number of 255ths, so exact values carry up to half a level of rounding (0.002); where a number needed more digits the output was scaled before reading (rows 19 and 20).
