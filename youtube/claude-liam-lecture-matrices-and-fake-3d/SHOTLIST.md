# Shot list: Matrices and Fake 3D

**What this is.** One row per body beat: what the sentence is about, the picture chosen, the lane that came second, and why this one won. Every beat was auditioned against at least one other lane.

**Why.** The lecture skill's BEST-BEAT LAW: no lane is the default and none has a quota; the picture whose motion is the claim wins. A reviewer can disagree with any row without reading code.

**Found.** 41 body beats. Lane histogram: manim 22, capture 15, remotion 4. Every Godot render shown is a Shader Lab capture of a tested file in `lab-shaders/`; the film shows no editor frame. Three shared pieces and four film-local things must exist before the build (below).

## Must exist before the build

Shared (specs in `../_godot-shaders-series/COMPONENT-SPECS.md`; built once, by whichever film is built first):

1. **`ShaderLab`** Remotion component (code left, a moving Godot render right; `layout` side, stack, video or code; `cues`, `readouts`, `rings`, `caption`, `title`). This film also uses its `code` layout alone (B37) and its `caption` line as a one-line definition (B11, B13, B35).
2. **`shader_kit.py`** Manim helpers (`vertex_quad`, `code_card`, `pixel_grid`, `uv_square`).
3. **Capture harness** `lab/capture.gd` + `capture_shader.sh` as the spec describes it (`--animate` schedules, `--scene`, `--material-at`, stills, keyframes as fractions of the clip with `--length`). This film adds only a scene script for the card (the `card` texture drawn at 1024 px, code in `evidence/planning-checks/runner-card.gd`; the unit factor U; the origin cross and hinge dot markers; the two panels of B22) and eased breakpoints (`CAPTURE-PLAN.md`, "What the harness needs for this film").

Film-local (write them in this film's `scenes.py`, next to the scenes):

4. **`screen_plane`**: a grid in Godot's directions, **x to the right and y DOWN** (Manim y = minus game y), with small axis labels; a warp helper that applies a two-by-two table to it (convert the table with y flipped: M' = F M F, F = diag(1, -1)).
5. **`matrix_table`**: a bracketed grid (two vertical bracket lines, ink, stroke 4); every entry its own `Text` (EB Garamond, at least 40 at 2x2, at least 34 at 3x3) so one row or column can be outlined; a ring is a terracotta `RoundedRectangle` outline (no fill, no ink ring over text).
6. **`card`**: a small cream rectangle with a warm grey outline, a teal arrow and a teal corner square drawn from shapes (the same card as in the captures); used in B23, B30, B36, B39.
7. **`top_view`**: the end-on view of B27 and B33 (screen line, end-on axis marker, a turning segment or square, shadow dots or bars).

**Docs screenshot** D1 for B05 via Playwright (see `CAPTURE-PLAN.md`).
