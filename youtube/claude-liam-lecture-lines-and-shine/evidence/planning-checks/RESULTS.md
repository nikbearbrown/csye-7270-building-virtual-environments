# Planning checks: results

**What this is.** The runs behind the numbers in `FACTCHECK.md`, in the order they were done, with the script, the log and what it means. All on Bear's Mac: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot 4.7.2 stable (official), Forward+ unless a line says Compatibility, 2026-10-13.
**Why.** So any figure on screen can be re-run. Failed and inconclusive runs are kept and labelled.
**Found.** One re-runnable script covers all 13 shown shaders in 131 Lab calls and 17 two-panel scene runs (`run_lab_checks.py`: 0 failed checks and no non-zero exit in its final run), one arithmetic script, one GDScript check and one scene script. Four findings changed the film: the undivided band gets **thinner**, not thicker, as the line steepens; the divided band does **not** break at ninety degrees, only the sign swaps; a centre shift moves the line by an angle-dependent amount; and a first-attempt `mix` leaks onto transparent pixels. Five runs did not give the answer first time and are kept.

Re-run everything: `python3 run_lab_checks.py` (about five minutes when the Lab is free; the Lab serialises runs, so other builders can make it slower), then `python3 arithmetic_checks.py` and `python3 make_contact_sheet.py`. Outputs: `measurements.txt` (every measured number with PASS or FAIL), `lab-results.jsonl` (one line per Lab call: shader, arguments, exit code, the full RESULT), `png/` (the 640 x 360 stills the capture plan refers to), `contact-sheet.png`, `run-1-verify1.log`, `arithmetic.txt`.

Framing used throughout (the framing the captures will use): a 640 x 360 Lab window; a white 128 px sprite at scale 2.25, so the test card is **288 x 288 px at (176, 36)**; band widths are given as a percent of the card width, so they do not depend on capture size. The blob is the Lab's teal test sprite. Pixel probes are taken at pixel centres computed from card addresses.

Commands (all from this folder; paths shortened). The Lab, once per shader and setting, exactly as logged in `lab-results.jsonl`:
`../../../_godot-shaders-series/lab/check_shader.sh ../../lab-shaders/05-band-true.gdshader --kind sprite --sprite-tex white --size 640x360 --set angle=45 --set thickness=0.1 --probe 0.5,0.5 --png png/D-true-45.png [--renderer gl_compatibility]`.
The scene script: `godot --path <scratch project> --script res://two_panels.gd -- --cfg <config.json>` (the configs are the `cfg` field of the `scene` lines in `lab-results.jsonl`). The GDScript check: `godot --path <scratch project> --script res://verify1.gd` (log: `run-1-verify1.log`). The scratch project is `project.godot` plus copies of the two scripts and `10-shine.gdshader`, in `/private/tmp/claude-501/godot-shaders-lines-and-shine/proj` (`run_lab_checks.py` creates it).

## Failed, wrong and inconclusive runs (kept)

- `run-0-first-attempts-transcribed.txt`: the first bit-reading shader did not compile (`>>` needs unsigned counts), and the second attempt printed the same number for every setting because zsh did not split `--set mode=4` into two arguments. Inconclusive and wrong as a set; redone, and the constant-1.0 self-test now runs first.
- `run-0-first-full-run-3-FAILs.txt`: the first complete run. Three FAILs, all mine: a step-order probe aimed at a pixel that is on the line, and two strict renderer comparisons that failed on one level of 255. Fixed in the script; the later run is clean.
- `run-1-verify1.log` T2: a run that is supposed to fail (a tween on a uniform never set from code) and does.
- An earlier tween run read the centre white 0.385 to 0.427 s after the call; the final run read 0.388 to 0.429 s. Frame timing is real-time dependent, so the documents quote the window (about 0.39 to 0.43 s).
- The Lab window clamps to the screen height: a 3840 x 2160 request gave a 3840 x 2004 window, while the card kept its 1728 px size (the measurements use the card, not the window).

## Notes for the series (see the final report)

The smooth warm and cool map is not renderer-independent to the last level of 8 bits; flat colours are. The `COLOR`-pre-filled-with-the-texture-color fact (docs and run) is not in the glossary's `COLOR` row.
