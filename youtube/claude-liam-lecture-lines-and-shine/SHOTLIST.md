# Shot list: Lines and Shine

**What this is.** One row per body beat: what the sentence is about, the picture chosen, the lane that came second, and why this one won. Every beat was auditioned against at least one other lane.

**Why.** The lecture skill's BEST-BEAT LAW: no lane is the default and none has a quota; the picture whose motion is the claim wins. A reviewer can disagree with any row without reading code.

**Found.** 35 body beats. Lane histogram: capture 21, manim 14. Every Godot render shown is a Shader Lab or scene capture of a tested file in `lab-shaders/`; no editor frames and no docs screenshots are needed. Four things must exist before the build (below).

## Must exist before the build

1. **`ShaderLab`** Remotion component (spec: `../_godot-shaders-series/COMPONENT-SPECS.md`; this film also uses its `caption` prop for one-line definitions and a `ramp.period`). 2. **`shader_kit.py`** Manim helpers (same file; this film uses `uv_square`, `plot` and plain Manim lines, arcs, dots and MathTex). 3. **Capture harness** `lab/capture.gd` + `capture_shader.sh` (same file), plus the two-panel scene `evidence/planning-checks/two_panels.gd` (provided, verified as stills) for the seven compare captures. 4. **Fourteen Manim scenes** named in the rows below.
