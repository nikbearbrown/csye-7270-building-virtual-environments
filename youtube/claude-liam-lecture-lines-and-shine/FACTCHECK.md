# Fact-check (Gate F): Lines and Shine

**What this is.** Every checkable claim in the narration and on screen, with its verdict and where the verdict comes from. Verdicts: PASS (checked), CORRECTED (the source or the brief said something looser or wrong and the film says the corrected thing), EXEMPT (a definition, a recap or a teaching frame, labelled as such), OPEN (cannot be verified yet; says why).
**Why.** A shader is where "it runs" and "it looks right" are furthest apart, and the material this film follows (one third-party video, known only through the content brief) is loose in three places. Nothing here is trusted because it was said confidently; every number was read from a real Godot 4.7.2 window or derived in `evidence/planning-checks/arithmetic.txt`.
**Found.** 71 rows: 62 PASS, 3 CORRECTED, 6 EXEMPT, 0 OPEN. Three things were learned by running, not by reading: the undivided band gets thinner, not thicker, as the line steepens (rows 30 and 31); the divided band does NOT break at ninety degrees, only the sign swaps (rows 22 to 25); and a centre shift moves the line by an angle-dependent amount (rows 41 and 42). Failed or surprising runs are kept (`run-0-*.txt`; the tween run that must fail, row 52).

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Godot **4.7.2 stable (official)**, Forward+ unless stated (Compatibility where stated). Docs: Godot 4.7 (docs.godotengine.org/en/stable), read 2026-10-13. Evidence in `evidence/planning-checks/`: `run_lab_checks.py` (writes `measurements.txt`, `lab-results.jsonl`, `png/`), `arithmetic_checks.py` (`arithmetic.txt`), `verify1.gd` (`run-1-verify1.log`), `two_panels.gd`, `scratch-shaders/`. In the Lab the test card is a 288 x 288 px square in a 640 x 360 frame; widths are given as a percent of the card.
