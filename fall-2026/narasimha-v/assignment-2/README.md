# Assignment 2 — Generate Art, Sound, and Music for Your Game

**Narasimha Reddy Valam · CSYE 7270, Fall 2026**

The game is **HOUSEGHOST**, and all of its work lives in one folder:

### → [`walker-houseghost-narasimha-v/`](walker-houseghost-narasimha-v/)

You are the ghost of a boy the world believes ran away, in the house a new family has just moved into. Flipping between the house as they see it and the house as you remember it lets you reach across and be seen — but every contact tears a day off the calendar toward the anniversary of the night you "ran," and the only adult who believes in ghosts is the man who made you one.
