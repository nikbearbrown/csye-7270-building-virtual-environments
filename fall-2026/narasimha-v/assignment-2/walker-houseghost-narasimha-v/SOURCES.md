**Scaling method, recorded because it caused a real bug:** every sheet was first scaled by figure height, which made the jump poses twice the size of the walking boy and the ghost 1.85 times. Height is meaningless across poses — a crouching child the same pixel height as a standing one is a far larger character. All sets are now matched to one body size using a pose-invariant measure: torso width for upright poses, and head width for the upside-down ghost, where the torso band lands on the legs instead. Final sizes are within 3 to 5 percent of the walking boy.


**Delivery format.** Every sound and both music tracks were mastered as 16-bit WAV and are shipped as Ogg Vorbis (`ffmpeg -c:a vorbis`, q8 for music, q6 for effects). Two reasons: the assignment names OGG or WAV and notes that Godot imports OGG with loop settings directly, and the course repository's root `.gitignore` excludes `*.wav`, so WAV masters cannot be posted. The conversion takes 22 MB to 1.2 MB with no change in duration. The WAV masters are retained locally as the originals; they are not in the repository because the course rule excludes them.

**Durations were set by how often a sound fires, not by how important it is.** Flip, jump and land play constantly, so they are short — a long sound on a repeated event drags behind the action and exhausts the ear. Contact and correct mark story beats and fire rarely, so they were given room to ring out.

**Edits, all by Claude with ffmpeg:** leading silence stripped below −50 dB so each sound fires on the frame of its event rather than slightly late; loudness normalised; a limiter at −0.5 dB. Measured on arrival, `sfx_land` and `sfx_jump` were 30 dB quieter than `sfx_flip` and would have been inaudible, so they were given +18 dB and +8 dB respectively before normalising. All six now peak within 2 dB of each other, and the balance between them is set in the engine instead (`LEVELS` in `audio.gd`): story beats forward, footsteps well back at −15 and −17 dB because they fire constantly.

**Verified** by the automated check, seven assertions, zero failures: one sound per flip, twelve flip calls during one turn still producing one sound, two contact resolves inside the cooldown producing one, one frost sound per day torn, a held jump producing one jump sound and one landing sound, and muting still counting the event because audio only observes state.

## The full sound set (nine)

The assignment asks for four events. The slice has nine, because the audio is doing narrative work rather than just confirming input.

| Asset | Fires on | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| `sfx_flip.ogg` | Turning the world over | ElevenLabs |
| `sfx_contact.ogg` | Touching a relic | ElevenLabs |
| `sfx_frost.ogg` | Each day torn off the calendar | ElevenLabs |
| `sfx_correct.ogg` | The loud way to be seen: the house notices | ElevenLabs |
| `sfx_land.ogg` | Landing from a jump | ElevenLabs |
| `sfx_jump.ogg` | Pushing off the ground | ElevenLabs |
| `sfx_step.ogg` | Each heel strike, **in the memory only** | ElevenLabs — prompt: "A single soft footstep of a child's bare foot on a thin worn carpet over old floorboards: a quiet dull press with a faint creak of the board beneath it. Close, small, light, dry. One step only, not a sequence." Trimmed to its transient at 0.080–0.230 s after measurement showed the energy lived in a 16 ms window, then compressed hard |
| `sfx_drift.ogg` | Continuously while the ghost moves | ElevenLabs — prompt: "A continuous low airy breath moving through a cold empty room, like air displaced by something passing. Soft, hollow, unbroken… Seamless and even throughout so it can loop." Levelled flat rather than compressed, then crossfaded 0.5 s of its own tail over its head to loop |
| `sfx_heart.ogg` | Under everything, faster and louder as the days run out | **Synthesised in code** by Claude — two exponentially decaying sine thuds at falling pitch, 78→46 Hz and 66→40 Hz, 310 ms apart. Not generated: a heartbeat is a shape arithmetic gets exactly right and a generator does not. Satisfies no generative requirement |

**The one that carries the story:** the boy has footsteps and the ghost has none. Walking in the memory produces a heel strike per stride; as the ghost there are no footsteps at all, only displaced air that starts when he moves and stops when he stops. Nothing in the game states that he is weightless — the audio simply stops pretending he is not.

**Why the earlier sounds could not be heard, and what fixed it.** Measured after the first pass, `contact` was audible for 19 percent of its length, `correct` for 15 and `frost` for 29: brief ticks surrounded by silence, which disappears entirely under a score at full level. The automated check had passed throughout, because counting that an event fired proves nothing about whether a person can hear it. All of them were compressed so they are dense rather than spiky — contact now 84 percent, correct 77, flip 96 — and the music steps back 15 dB under each one.

## The child, and the rest of the late additions

| Asset | Origin | Notes |

| `child_1..3.png` | **ChatGPT image generation**, chatgpt.com, 2026-10-07 | Prompt: a girl of about six, the new family's daughter, "not a ghost, solid, healthy and ordinary," in a bright yellow raincoat she refuses to take off indoors, mismatched socks, holding a stuffed rabbit with one ear missing. | `sfx_heart.ogg` | **Synthesised in code** | Two exponentially decaying sine thuds at falling pitch, 78→46 Hz and 66→40 Hz, 310 ms apart. Satisfies no generative requirement |

**Colour matching, recorded because it was a real defect.** The walk and jump sheets came back graded 30 percent brighter and much cooler than the idle drawing — measured, RGB 79/74/68 against 68/59/44 — so the boy visibly turned pale the moment he moved. Both sets were matched to the idle per channel, which leaves the artwork untouched and corrects only the grade.

**Scale matching, likewise.** Three measures were tried and failed before one worked. Figure height fails across poses, because a crouching child the same pixel height as a standing one is a far larger character. Shoulder width fails because raised arms narrow it. Head width fails on the rising jump pose, because the topmost pixels there are his raised hands rather than his head. Body area, which barely changes when a figure crouches or stretches, is what finally held — and the idle was ultimately replaced by the walk cycle's own legs-together frame, because no scaling reconciles a standing pose with a gait that never stands that straight.

## Written content

The opening lines, the six notes placed through the house and the two endings are **written by me with Claude**, not generated by any model. They were revised once after two readers found the first version unclear: *I need one of them to see me* never stated what being seen would achieve, and leaned on a pronoun pointing back a line. The replacement gives each line a single idea and makes the last one carry the reason.

They are listed here because the asset log should account for everything a player encounters, and because the distinction matters: no language model wrote the fiction, and no generative model produced any text that appears on screen.

## Storyboard panels

**The colour rule.** The panels are monochrome grey ink and graphite except for two deliberate exceptions, and the rule is the game's own logic rather than a decoration: **grey is the truth, amber is the memory, yellow is the one living person who can see him.** Measured across the finished set, panel 1 and panel 6 contain no colour at all, amber appears only where the boy reaches into the past (panels 2, 3 and 5, draining out in 5 as the memory betrays him), and the only yellow in all six panels is the child's raincoat in panel 4, at about 1 % of the frame.
