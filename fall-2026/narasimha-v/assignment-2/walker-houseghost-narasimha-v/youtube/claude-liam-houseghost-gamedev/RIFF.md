# Riff notes — commentary grounded in what was inspected

Each line below was written after looking at the thing, not at the idea of it.

- **The order rule.** `flip()` changes state, emits, then plays. Audio can be deleted
  from this project and nothing about what it does changes. That is the test.
- **The guard is the feature.** `_flipping` is two words of code and it is the reason
  mashing the key cannot stack a sound. The automated check exists to prove it.
- **One vector.** The inversion is `up_direction`. No camera rotation, no mirrored
  scene, no second level. The cheapest possible implementation of the whole premise.
- **Matched peaks are not matched loudness.** The mix table is the clearest case in
  the project of a comment and the code disagreeing, with the comment being believed.
- **The importer lies quietly.** `loop` defaults to false on Ogg. Nothing errors. The
  score simply stops, once, and only a person listening would notice.
- **The obstacle is the fiction.** `solid_in_memory` means his furniture is in the
  way of the boy who still lives there. Walking past it requires giving that up.
- **The meter is a child.** Three poses carry what a number would have carried, and
  the difference is that you feel the second one.
