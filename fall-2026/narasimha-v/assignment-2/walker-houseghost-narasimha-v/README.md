# HOUSEGHOST

> You are the ghost. They are the new family. The house remembers what everyone agreed to forget.

**Project:** `walker-houseghost-narasimha-v` · CSYE 7270 Assignment 2 — Generate Art, Sound, and Music for Your Game
**Author:** Narasimha Reddy Valam · **Engine:** Godot 4 (slice pending; version pinned at first scene commit)
**Started from:** an empty Godot 4 project. The world-inversion verb is carried forward as an *idea* from my Assignment 1 game ([walker-jumpman-narasimha-v](https://github.com/narasimhareddyvalam/walker-jumpman-narasimha-v)); no code or assets are reused.

You play the ghost of a boy the world believes ran away, in the house a new family just moved into. Flip between the house as they see it (upright, bare, theirs) and the house as you remember it (upside down, lamplit, yours) to reach across and be seen — but every contact tears a day off the calendar toward the anniversary of the night you "ran," and the only adult who believes in ghosts is the man who made you one.
