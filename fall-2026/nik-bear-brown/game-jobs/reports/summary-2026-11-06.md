# Summary report: game, real-time 3D and virtual-environment job listings, 2026-11-06

## Executive summary

**What this is.** A dated summary of 792 active job listings from 27 companies, collected to show what a master's student interested in games, real-time 3D or virtual environments could apply for, so the CSYE 7270 course (and any follow-on classes) can be designed around real roles. Families, skills, seniority and years are **AI-made proposals** from rules and a word list; 0 of 792 have been confirmed by the course designer.

**Why read it.** It is a small sample from named company boards, not the market. It shows which kinds of jobs exist, which tools and skills their text names, and which of those the course materials do not mention.

**What it found.**
- The run read 2,378 postings from 35 company boards and kept 792 after rules for role family, game or 3D relevance and a 120-day window.
- Largest role families: software engineering general 203, production product 135, data ml ai 101, art animation 77, online backend 69.
- Engines named in the text: Unity in 107 (14%), Unreal Engine in 186 (23%), Godot in 2 (0%).
- Figma or design handoff appears in 32 (4%) of listings; with a named engine as well in 9.
- Early-career signal (an intern, new-grad, junior or associate title, or a stated minimum of 2 years or less): 144 (18%).

Records: `game-jobs/listings.jsonl` (append-only; take the last line per URL). Mentions mean the listing text uses a term, not that the role requires it.

## What limits these results

- **Coverage.** Only boards on Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever that this tool can read. Many studios use their own career sites, Workday or other systems and are not seen: the large console and PC publishers are mostly missing.
- **Title rules.** Roles are classified by title patterns. A title that does not say what the job is ("Engineer II") is classified from its words alone and may be wrong.
- **Windows.** Only postings from the last 120 days.
- **AI-made fields.** Family, skills, years, seniority and salary come from rules and word lists and can miss or over-match.
- **Not pushed or sent.** Files are local. `raw/` holds full listing text and stays out of version control.
