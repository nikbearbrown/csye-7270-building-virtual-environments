## The game in two sentences

Downfall is a single-player PC extraction ARPG with a top-down camera and real-time
combat. The player is Lappland, a Rhodes Island field operative, who takes a field
contract to one of three regions, fights and loots down floors that only go one way,
and must keep deciding whether to push deeper or extract before she is worn down.
(`策划案_v1_0.md` §一)

> **Rights note.** Lappland, Rhodes Island, PRTS, Closure and the regions belong to
> Arknights (Hypergryph). This breaks the assignment rule against copyrighted
> characters and brands. The author chose to keep the game as it is; see
> [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) §Rights.

## Core loop

The loop is 接单 → 推进 → 交付/失败 (`策划案_v1_0.md` §三):

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Accept | Pick a region at the Rhodes base and insure the gear you bring. |
| Advance | Clear a floor (real-time combat, loot, devices), then take one descent. |
| Deliver | Extract and keep everything. |
| Fail | Die and keep only protected items. |

- **Repeated action:** clear a floor, then take the next descent.
- **Decision each time:**
  - which of three routes (low, mid or high danger; −3 / 0 / +5 hidden danger);
  - which of three relics at a boost device;
  - whether to extract now (random extraction on floors 4–6, fixed at the camp every 10 floors) or keep going.
- **Risk:**
  - progress is one-way;
  - unprotected backpack loot is lost on death;
  - danger rises each floor: `D = 3.5×(floor−1) + route` (`撤离与营地修订案_v1_0.md` §4).

## Design pillars

These are taken from `策划案_v1_0.md` §九 and `界面策划案_v1_0.md` §1. Each pillar has
one asset choice that serves it.

| Pillar | Asset choice that honors it |
|---|---|
| **Combat is won by positioning and timing** ("战斗靠走位和时机取胜") | Every enemy attack has a four-frame telegraph (startup → charge → glint → release). Each normal enemy also has its own short attack cue. Only elites get a red ground circle. |
| **Every descent is a trade-off** ("每一次深入都要有取舍") | The route node strip marks danger by colour. The settlement screen outlines each backpack item by whether it was kept, insured or lost. |
| **Region differences are perceivable** ("地区之间的差异能被感知") | There are three generated ground surfaces and prop groups. The mine has ore and timber supports, the city has broken walls and shopfronts, and the snowfield has trees and frozen rock. |
| **Reading attacks comes first** ("读招优先") | Ground surfaces are kept low-contrast "so characters and red combat warning areas stand out" (`art/field/prompts.md`). No HUD sits in the central 640×300 of the screen. |

## Art direction

All art is pixel art, drawn in an "illustrated pixel art" manner meant to look like
Arknights base illustrations. The world renders at 640×360 with nearest-neighbour
upscale, so the illustrated feel has to come from technique rather than resolution
(`罗德岛基地美术策划案_v1_0.md` §3.5). Lighting does the mood work: dark-steel interiors
with cool screen glow, and dim, low-contrast field surfaces under hard red danger
colour.

Reference notes, in words:

- **Materials:** white or light-grey plastic shells over dark metal; worn industrial timber and ore in the mine; snow-dusted rock.
- **Lighting:** emissive screens and additive pools of light in front of equipment; walls darker toward the top.
- **Colour discipline:**
  - originium orange is used only for contamination and raw ore;
  - blue `#0098dc` means confirm;
  - red means enemies and elites;
  - near-black outline on at most 3% of pixels.

## Audio direction

The design asks that normal physical attacks be readable "by motion, facing and
sound" (`策划案_v1_0.md` §五). In practice this means short, distinct cues per enemy
type, plus feedback for hit, hurt, loot, build choice, charge-ready and extraction.

- **What exists:** 13 short cues synthesized in code (`game/field_audio.gd`). **None of them is generated by a model.**
- **Music:** none. No design document says when music should play, change or stop. Phase 7 (`策划案_v1_0.md` §十) still lists "补齐提示、音效" (fill in cues and sound effects) as open work.

### Update 2026-10-07 (later the same day): audio generated

> Added after the paragraph above was written; that paragraph is kept as it was.

Generated audio now exists and is wired in, made with Gemini's music mode (see
[SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) §Generated audio):

- **Base:** MUS-BASE plays in the base. It should feel calm, high-tech and warm: home between contracts.
- **Contracts:** MUS-MINE plays during a contract. It is tense and low-key, and leaves room for the enemy cues.
- **When it changes or stops:**
  - pausing in the field freezes the music on the same sample;
  - pausing in the base ducks it by 10 dB;
  - death fades the field track and plays STING-FAIL once;
  - extraction plays STING-EXTRACT once;
  - base music returns only after either sting has finished.
- **Warehouse events:** the lights coming on and going off have generated sounds. The hatch and shelf events are counted but stay silent until their files exist.

The 13 synthesized combat and UI cues are unchanged.
