## Format

- Same style as the material and gear icons in `art/items/runtime/`: thick outline, flat fill, transparent background. Don't limit the palette or the number of colours.
- Size by footprint, like the gear: 1 cell = 32 px, 2 cells = 72 px, 3 = 112, 4 = 152, 5 = 192, 6 = 232 (so 1×2 = 32×72, 2×2 = 72×72).
- Delivery: `art/items/runtime/<id>.png`. Ids are already wired in `ItemIcons.COLLECTIBLES` and the case/gift lookup; no code change needed.
- Each must be recognisable as the object named, and each region's set should feel like its place (Ursus mining town, Lungmen street, Sami tundra, Siracusan family house).

## 整理箱 (cases)

Four kinds × four levels. Ids `case_<kind>_<level>`: kinds `material`, `collectible`, `potion`, `gear`; levels 1–4. Footprints: level 1 = 2×2 (72×72), 2 = 2×3 (72×112), 3 = 3×4 (112×152), 4 = 5×6 (192×232).

- One family look (Rhodes Island storage case, dark grey with a coloured band and latch), the kind told by its band colour and a small symbol: material = amber band + bolt; collectible = gold band + small statuette; potion = white band + green cross; gear = steel-blue band + crossed blades.
- Levels grow sturdier: level 1 a canvas-and-frame crate, 2 a plastic hard case, 3 a metal case with corner guards, 4 a large wheeled locker.
