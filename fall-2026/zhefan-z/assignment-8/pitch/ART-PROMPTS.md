# ART-PROMPTS — generated concept art for the pitch

All images in `pitch/art/` were generated by me with **Gemini 4 in the Gemini app** (free access through my Google account) on 2026-11-20, under Assignment 2's rules for generated art: no named living artist, brand or copyrighted character in any prompt. They are concept art for the final project's story moments, **not in the game yet**, and the film labels them "Generated (Gemini 4) · concept art, not in the game". The prompts were drafted by claude.ai from my requests unless marked as mine.

**Reference given to Gemini:** my Assignment 2 pixel sprites, `walker-magic-zhefan/assets/sprites/mage/mage-contact-sheet-4x.png` and `assets/sprites/wolf/wolf-contact-sheet-4x.png` (Assignment 7, `840b501`).

## The witch

1. **Character illustration** (prompt drafted by claude.ai):

   > A 2D anime-style character portrait for a game's dialogue box, half-body, facing slightly to the right, portrait orientation 3:4. Use the attached pixel sprites only as the character reference: a young witch girl with long white twin tails tied with red ribbons, red eyes, a tall blue witch hat with a red band and a gold buckle, a blue short cape over a brown dress, holding a wooden staff topped with a red gem in a ring. Expression: calm and determined, a small confident smile. Clean line art and soft cel shading, warm rim light from the left as if from firelight. Plain flat solid background in a single color (#00FF00 bright green) so it can be cut out. No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark.

   Gemini returned full-body illustrations instead. The first versions had a glowing halo above her head, which I removed with the edit below; those first versions were not kept.

2. **Halo removed** (edit, drafted by claude.ai):

   > Remove the glowing halo ring above her head completely. Keep everything else exactly the same: the character, pose, expression, outfit, staff, colours and background.

3. **A more experienced look** (my own follow-up request in the same conversation): I asked Gemini to make her look like a more experienced adventurer. The exact wording was not kept. The two full-body images used here (smiling, and talking with an open hand) come from this request.

4. **Expression sheet** (drafted by claude.ai), with the full-body image as the reference:

   > Using the attached image as the exact character reference, create ONE image: a 2×2 expression sheet of the same girl, four bust-up portraits (head to just above the waist) in a grid, all the same size, framing and angle, the same outfit, hat, hair, colours and art style as the reference. Only the facial expression changes: Top left: CURIOUS — just stepped into an unknown cave: eyes wide and bright, eyebrows slightly raised, lips slightly parted in a small "oh", head tilted a little. Top right: DETERMINED — ready to face a dangerous enemy: eyebrows lowered and drawn together, sharp focused eyes, mouth closed in a firm line. Bottom left: VICTORY — joyful: a big bright open-mouthed smile, eyes curved shut with happiness, cheeks blushing. Bottom right: SAD — defeated: eyebrows tilted up in the middle, teary eyes looking down, mouth turned down, one tear on her cheek. Plain flat solid bright green background (#00FF00) behind each portrait, with clear gaps between the four. No halo, no text, no labels, no letters, no logo, no watermark.

   The sheet used here has a green background.

## The great wolf

5. **Wolf** (drafted by claude.ai), with the wolf pixel sprite and the witch's expression sheet as references:

   > Create ONE image: a side-by-side 2-panel expression sheet of a giant wolf, the final boss of an indie game, drawn as bust-up dialogue portraits (head, neck and shoulders) in the same soft anime cel-shaded style, line art and lighting as the attached witch portraits. Use the attached pixel-art wolf only as the design reference for its shape and fur colours; this is the same wolf, much larger and older, the ruler of a dark cave. The wolf faces toward the left side of the image, as if speaking to someone standing to its left. Thick grey-blue fur with darker streaks, a scarred muzzle, glowing eyes, a heavy ruff of fur around the neck. Left panel: MENACING, before the fight: lips pulled back showing fangs, ears forward, eyes glowing bright amber, a low threatening look. Right panel: DEFEATED, after the fight: ears lowered, eyes half-closed and dim, head bowed, calm and tired, no longer hostile. Both panels the same size, framing and angle. Plain flat solid bright green background (#00FF00) behind each, with a clear gap between them. No text, no labels, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no blood.

   Gemini returned two separate full-body images (a calm wolf and a snarling one) with glowing cyan eyes; the versions used here have green backgrounds, and I kept them as they are.
