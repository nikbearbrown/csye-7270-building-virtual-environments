# claude-liam-walker-survival-shooting-a7-gamedev

Text records for the Assignment 7 (animation) godot-gamedev film, walker mode, narrated by Liam (local Kokoro am_onyx). Media files are not stored here.

- beat_sheet.json - beats, timings, narration, visuals
- NARRATION.md - the narration script and the prompts used
- SHOTLIST.md - what is on screen in each beat and its label
- FACTCHECK.md - every claim and its source
- CAPTURE.md - fixtures reused and the new scripted-input capture, with its timeline
- gamedev-evidence.json - hashes of the film, clips and media; code/result pairs
- capture-tools/ - the capture script; build.py.txt - the film builder

Placeholders: <course-repo> (the course repository), <film-work> (the film work folder), <film-out> (finished films), <brutalist.art> (the course toolkit).

Built under a hard deadline with a simplified pipeline (PIL cards + ffmpeg) instead of the toolkit's Remotion scenes; see FACTCHECK and CAPTURE for limits.
