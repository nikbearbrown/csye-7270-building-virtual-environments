## Two clocks

Measured (60 fps, `--fixed-fps`). Before TUNING r1: the press is read on physics frame N; the state machine entered `raise_1h` through the `hip -> raise_1h` expression in that frame's process step, whose `reset` left the clip at 0.0 for a second process frame; the clip ended and switched to `hold_1h` in the process step of N + 41, and `Humanoid` read it on N + 42 (0.033 s after clip frame 20's time). After TUNING r1 (kept; `Humanoid` starts `raise_1h` on the press frame): clip frame f is read on N + 1 + 2f and "raise finished" on N + 41. Largest disagreement now: 1 physics frame = 0.017 s at 60 fps, the process-tick clip read on the physics tick, which is what the brief's two-clocks section first estimated. 30 fps Movie Maker: lit on movie frame 55 before r1, 54 after.
