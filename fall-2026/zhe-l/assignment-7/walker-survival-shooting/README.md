# walker-survival-shooting — Assignment 7

A third-person 3D extraction shooter. The player lives in a subway base, carries a rugged military tablet, goes out to fight monsters and extracts. This assignment (Assignment 7) adds two animations: a **pixel head in the tablet OS taskbar** (sprite sheet, loops) and **the main character raising the tablet from her hip with one hand** (imported rig, a committing action with startup / active / recovery phases).

## Starting point and versions
- **Starting point:** my working project after Assignment 6 (with the tablet OS and the new tablet model), comments translated into English (A7's first commit, 8d1c8a73). `godot/shaders/pad_base_light.gdshader` keeps my own Chinese comments (an exception for this assignment).
- **Godot:** 4.7.2-stable (Windows), renderer **Forward+**, D3D12, Jolt Physics. Window 2048 × 1152.
- **Blender:** 5.2.2 (only for keying the rig clip and exporting the `.glb`).
- **Claude Code:** Claude Opus 5.5.

## How to run
1. Open `godot/project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2; import once first: `godot --headless --path godot --import`. In a fresh clone the first import prints errors for the two `.translation` files (they are generated by that same import); a second import is clean.
2. Run the main scene `base.tscn` (F5).

**Controls:** WASD to move, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, right mouse button to aim, Tab to take out / switch / put away the tablet (short press: put away → one hand, one hand → put away, two hands → one hand; long press of 0.4 s: put away → two hands, one hand → two hands, two hands → put away), F to use the wall light switch (only with the tablet put away), V to toggle vsync (debug key).

**Seeing the two clips:**
- **The one-handed raise:** with the tablet put away, press Tab briefly. The left hand reaches to the left hip, takes the upright tablet, lifts it and turns it to face her, in about 0.67 s (20 frames at 30 fps); during the raise she cannot walk, run or jump, and the screen lights only when it is over. Pressing Tab again during the raise interrupts it: a short press puts it back on the hip (the screen never lights), a long press blends from the current pose to two hands. Turn the camera to see her from the front.
- **The pixel head:** with the tablet out, look at the far left of the tablet OS taskbar (on the held tablet and on the demo tablet at the bottom right). 8 frames at 4 fps, one loop every 2 s: the head bobs 1 pixel and blinks once as it sinks.
- **Preview scenes (test fixtures):** `godot/previews/tablet_raise_preview.tscn` (loads the real base, presses Tab briefly on rendered frame 30, prints one log line per frame; add `-- --front` to turn the player's camera 180 degrees in game) and `godot/previews/pixel_head_preview.tscn` (the head enlarged 6×), for the frame strips.

## How to run the tests
Every test runs through `scripts/run-test.sh <test file> <fps|realtime> [timeout]`. It always runs headless, adds a timeout, pins the frame rate, keeps a log in `logs/`, and fails on `SHADER ERROR`, `SCRIPT ERROR` or `Failed loading resource`. Set `GODOT` to the Godot console executable if `godot_console` / `godot` is not on `PATH`.

```bash
./scripts/run-test.sh a3_asset_check.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh screen_glow_check.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_landing_dust_config.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_landing_dust_events.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_light_dust_config.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_light_dust_state.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_tablet_pins.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_pixel_head_clip.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_tablet_raise_clip.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_tablet_state_machine.gd 60
./scripts/run-test.sh test_tablet_raise_edges.gd 60
```

Expected PASS counts: 55 / 29 / 157 / 27 / 110 / 41 / 38 / 37 / 25 / 26 / 126, all exit 0. Mutations use `scripts/mutate.sh`, each in its own sparse git worktree. Details, frame strips and revisions are in `TEST-REPORT.md` and `TUNING.md`.

Frame strips are captured in a real window with Movie Maker (the output folder must exist first; otherwise no frames are written and there is no error), for example:

```bash
godot_console --path godot res://previews/tablet_raise_preview.tscn --write-movie "$(pwd -W)/captures/tablet_raise/r2-front/frame.png" --fixed-fps 30 --quit-after 90 -- --front > logs/tablet_raise_r2-front.log

```

## What this assignment added
- **Sprite clip, the pixel head** `godot/computer_system/tablet_os/system/pixel_head/`: `pixel_head.tscn` (a `Sprite2D`, `hframes` 8, nearest filtering; an `AnimationPlayer` clip `idle` with one discrete `frame` track, 8 keys 0–7, `LOOP_LINEAR`, 2.0 s, `step` 0.25), placed at the far left of the taskbar in `tablet_os.tscn`. The sheet `avatar-idle-front-blink-8x1-56.png` (448 × 56), its `.import` and `ATTRIBUTION.md` are in the same folder. **The head is AI-generated**: Codex's (OpenAI) image tool generated the head, Codex reduced it to a pixel grid and laid out the frames in code, and Claude added the blink at my request. The sheet as downloaded is in `design/avatar-sprite/`.
- **Rig clip, the one-handed raise** `godot/assets/characters/main_character/main_character.glb` (the commissioned main-character model, used with permission; see `ATTRIBUTION.md` beside it): three actions, `tablet_raise_one_hand` (frames 0–20, 30 fps, no loop), `tablet_hold_one_hand` and `tablet_hold_hip`; loop modes are set in each animation's settings in `.glb.import`. Exported from Blender with `tools/blender/export_main_character.py` (the Blender source file is not in this repo).
- **State machine:** the character's `AnimationTree` in `humanoid.tscn` (an `AnimationNodeStateMachine`: `hip` / `raise_1h` / `hold_1h` / `hold_2h`, 10 transitions advanced by the expression `get_tablet_hold() == N`, 0.2 s cross-fades). It is the one authority over the skeleton; the tablet hangs under a `BoneAttachment3D` on the `tablet` bone, so it is also the one authority over the tablet's position. The three old slots and the 0.35 s slide are removed. `humanoid.gd` starts the raise on the press frame (TUNING r1); during the raise `is_raising_tablet()` blocks movement, and `is_tablet_screen_lit()` decides when the screen lights.
- **Tests:** behaviour pins (committed before the animation change), two read-back tests, the state-machine structure test, edge tests (one frame before, on and after each phase boundary, short and long presses), `scripts/mutate.sh`.
- **Preview scenes and frame strips:** `godot/previews/`, `tools/make_strip.py`, `captures/` (fixture captures).
- **Documents:** `CLAUDE.md` (subagent draft plus my review), `CHANGE-BRIEF.md` (with dated revisions), `TUNING.md` (r0, r1, r2), `TEST-REPORT.md`, `../FRICTIONAL.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `prompt-1.txt`, `session-1.jsonl`.

## Known limitations
- **Only the one-handed raise is a keyed clip.** The other five switches (back to the hip, one hand ↔ two hands, hip ↔ two hands) are the state machine's 0.2 s cross-fades: the path is straight, and the hand and the tablet are interpolated separately, so they may come apart mid-blend. Lowering, the two-handed raise and the other clips are planned for later.
- **The two-handed hold pose is a placeholder** (it uses the one-handed pose), so in first person with two hands the tablet is far away.
- **The character stands still while raising** and can walk only once the clip is over. That is the brief's rule; I decided to change it later to walking while raising (it needs a walk clip and animation layers).
- **The 8 mm overshoot on frame 18 is almost gone in the game**: Godot's importer dropped the tablet bone's keys on frames 18–19. I judged the difference invisible and kept it (TUNING r0, "Where we differ").
- The screen lights 1 physics frame (1/60 s) after the raise ends: the clip advances on the process tick and the game reads it on the physics tick (TEST-REPORT, "Two clocks").
- On the hip, part of the tablet clips into the coat (clipping I accepted).
- To fit a long press inside the 7-frame startup, the edge tests lower the long-press time to 0.095 s.
- Landing-dust Effect A's `visibility_aabb` has been too small since A6 r1 (a limitation carried over from A6, not changed here).
- The game has no playable combat or extraction yet.
