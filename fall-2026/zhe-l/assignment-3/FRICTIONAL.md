### 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-15 — Assignment 3 brief, weapon crates, the furniture grid, my hand edit, computer desks

- **Date and what I was working on:** 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-15. Read the Assignment 3 requirements, gathered my assets into `design/item_resource/`, wrote the brief, made the third asset (two weapon crates), made my hand edit in Blender, and added two computer desks.
- **I tried / expected:** Reuse the chair and the tablet as two of the three assets, bring them within the course limits, and add a crate that opens and closes.
- **What happened:** Claude measured the existing files in headless Blender: the tablet had 804 triangles, over the 500 limit, and the chair (9 boxes) and the tablet (21 boxes) are both over the 6-shape limit. Claude's first brief draft left gaps and marked things as "chosen by Claude" that I had already decided (one cuboid and one cube crate; military green, from the tablet); I corrected that. The tablet went down to 388 triangles by keeping bevels only on the body and the corner bumpers. The first crates were 1.2 × 0.5 × 0.4 m and 0.5 m cubed, and their handles and latches stuck out, so the footprints were 1.26 × 0.515 and 0.56 × 0.515 m. After I set the grid, Claude rebuilt them so the whole crate fits 1.0 × 0.5 and 0.5 × 0.5 exactly. Godot 4.7.2 headless import of every `.glb`: node scale 1, 1, 1; the bottoms at y = 0 (the tablet at −0.081, as intended); the crate lids reach −100° after `open` and 0° after `close`. During one check Claude's script accidentally re-exported `test_chair.glb` over the file in the Godot project; it came out with the same size and dimensions.
- **What I did:** Made the decisions: get every asset under 500 triangles first; keep the tablet's origin in the centre because it does not affect holding it; the character is 1.6 m tall; the chair is the start-menu chair; the crates are buildable furniture that store items (small crate `small_box` 10 × 10, large crate `big_box` 20 × 10); the furniture grid is 1 cell = 0.1 m, so the large crate became 1.0 × 0.5 m. **My hand edit in Blender:** in Edit Mode I raised the small crate's two latches so each sits half on the lid and half on the body, so it reads as a latch that locks the lid, and moved both end handles up, because handles near the top give a lower centre of gravity when the crate is carried. Claude measured the result and found that the right latch had been stretched 0.10 m deep into the lid and the box, and that the two latches were about 2 mm off from each other. I fixed it myself and told Claude not to measure it again, but the fix never reached the saved files: when Claude collected numbers for the test report, the `.blend` and `.glb` still had the 0.10 m deep latch, while my log and asset log already said it was fixed. I fixed it a second time by deleting the right latch and duplicating the left one, then saved and exported. Claude measured again: the depth and height were right, the right latch is 6 mm off-centre compared with the left (I kept that; it does not matter for the design), and my duplicate had left a second latch hidden inside the left one, which Claude deleted at my request. I asked Claude to apply the same edit to the large crate. For the computer desks I chose a 0.7 m desktop, a monitor riser as the computer-desk feature, and dark-grey metal.
