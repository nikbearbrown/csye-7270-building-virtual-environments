# Explainer rubric — 25 points

CSYE 7270 · Fall 2026

**Reference example only.** The instructor subsequently specified that this course's graded assignments are worth 100 points each and occur every 10 days. Use the [100-point framework](100-point-assignment.md) for course assignments. This file preserves the supplied 25-point example and its explanation subcriteria; it is not a scheduled assignment.

**Course AI policy:** [AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz).

| Component | Points |
|---|---:|
| Explanation of the concept | 15 |
| Frictional — honest log | 2.5 |
| GitHub version posting matching Canvas | 2.5 |
| Relative Quartile | 5 |
| **Total** | **25** |

These are the syllabus's proportions (60 / 10 / 10 / 20 percent) applied to a 25-point total. Relative Quartile is exactly 20%.

## Explanation — 15 points

- The concept is correctly explained, and you can defend every claim in the video. **6 points**
- The mechanism is *shown*—motion, a worked example, or real output carries the idea rather than narration alone. **4 points**
- Numbers are real and reproducible; constructed illustrations are labeled as constructed. **3 points**
- You name one thing the explanation does not establish. **2 points**

## Frictional — 2.5 points

Provide the honest log described in [Frictional](../../prerequisites/assessment-policy.md#frictional--honest-log). Record real attempts, uncertainty, responses, learning, and human/AI contributions. It is not a polished performance of suffering, and an unsuccessful attempt can earn full credit.

## GitHub version posting matching Canvas — 2.5 points

Post the required source and documentation, identify the submitted commit in Canvas, and submit the matching version. Link large media from the README and identify its version. Follow [GitHub version posting matching Canvas](../../prerequisites/assessment-policy.md#github-version-posting-matching-canvas).

## Relative Quartile — 5 points

Compares specificity, substantive improvement, demonstrated understanding, evidence, honesty about verification, usability, and professional communication across the whole comparison group. Meeting the stated requirements earns the 20 criterion-based points; it does not guarantee these 5. The instructor and TAs review the full group before assigning it. Full criteria: [Relative Quartile](../../prerequisites/assessment-policy.md#relative-quartile).

Production polish is considered only as professional communication. It does not substitute for a correct explanation, and a plain video that teaches the concept precisely will outrank a beautiful one that does not.

## You must be able to explain it

You may use Claude throughout—for the script, the beat sheet, the code, the build. Name what it contributed in `SOURCES.md`.

You remain responsible for the submission. The instructor or a TA may ask you to explain any part of the video, the beat sheet, or the build. Being unable to explain your own submission reduces points under the relevant criteria, and misrepresenting what was verified is an academic integrity matter under the [course AI policy](../../prerequisites/assessment-policy.md#you-must-be-able-to-explain-it).
