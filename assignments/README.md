# Assignments — CSYE 7270, Fall 2026

Graded assignments are due **every 10 days**, worth **100 points each**, and include a required explainer video made with Brutalist's Godot skills. Canvas supplies the actual deadlines. Modules and book chapters correspond one-to-one; assignments follow their own 10-day cadence and identify the module material they cover.

Read [AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz), the [written course AI policy](../prerequisites/ai-policy.md), and the [Godot explainer requirements](../prerequisites/brutalist-godot-explainers.md).

The [100-point grading framework](rubrics/100-point-assignment.md) allocates 60 to implementation and explanation, 10 to Frictional, 10 to GitHub/Canvas version matching, and 20 to Relative Quartile.

Assignment 1 is drafted as [Assignment 1 - Extend Walker Jumpman](01-extend-walker-jumpman.md), with its own 100-point rubric. A separate Canvas HTML paste (generated locally from this Markdown; see [canvas/README.md](../canvas/README.md)) keeps the assignment apart from the Module 1 lesson. The instructor sets the actual deadline in Canvas. The current delivery uses manual pastes, not a new IMSCC. The 25-point rubric in this folder is a reference example, not a scheduled course assignment.

Its Canvas paste is generated locally from the Markdown; see [canvas/README.md](../canvas/README.md).
