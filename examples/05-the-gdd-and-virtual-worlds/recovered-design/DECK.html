02 — Vision

OBSERVED GAME-BRIEF.md
A blue robot moves through a textured 3D platform environment, jumps between surfaces, collects coins, and shoots projectiles at patrolling enemies. A camera follows the character and responds to obstructing geometry.

03 — Design Pillars INFERRED — requires human confirmation

A — Grounded Physical Movement
What it protects: character must feel mass and momentum cost
  • Jolt Physics 120 Hz
  • Gravity = 22.0 m/s² (2.24× Earth)
  • AIR_IDLE_DEACCEL = false
  • Air accel factor = 0.5
  • Sharp turn >140° breaks momentum
B — Readable Camera
What it protects: player sees action without fighting camera
  • Three-ray autoturn ±25°
  • Obstruction pull to collision point
  • Top-level detach (no parent artifact)
  • Height clamped [0, 2]
  • Autoturn speed: 50°/s
C — Inspectability
What it protects: every mechanism readable from source
  • 8 scripts, no AutoLoads
  • No cross-scene coupling
  • Feature logic in owning scene
  • Walker brief: inspect & verify
  • No singleton state managers
All three pillars inferred from code structure and GAME-BRIEF.md. Run /gdd v2 to define them explicitly with human input.

07 — Shoot, Collect, Reset

M-06 Shooting OBSERVED
  • Press-edge only (not held)
  • Bullet spawned as scene sibling
  • BULLET_SPEED = 20.0 m/s
  • bullet.enabled → false on hit
  • Prevents multi-hit while fading
  • SHOOT_TIME = 1.5 (anim blend)

  • Increments Player.coins
  • 73 instances in level
  • Label3D + 4 parallax copies
  • Session-only — no persistence
M-05 Fall Recovery OBSERVED
  • Trigger 1: y < -12
  • Trigger 2: reset_position (R)
  • → initial_position (from _ready)
  • → velocity = ZERO
  • → reset_physics_interpolation()
  • Tests verified both triggers

Three-ray logic

  • Right only → rotate camera left

15 — Open Questions MISSING → decisions.md

#QuestionStakes
OQ-01 What is the session goal? No win state, no level-complete, no score goal. Film cannot describe what success looks like
OQ-02 Who is the target player? Student, game-jam player, or film viewer? Determines scope, polish, and narration frame
OQ-03 What are the design pillars? Nothing in source declares them. No rejection criterion for future design changes
OQ-04 Can the player be harmed by enemies? Currently no. Removes all tension from the combat mechanic
OQ-05 Is 73-coin collection completable? No total shown; no trigger fires. Completion is invisible to the player
