### What controls the timing

- Hitbox on/off is controlled by code, not the attack animation tracks:
  - Entering `ATTACK` sets `visible` and `monitoring` true at [sword.gd:62](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/sword.gd:62).
  - Entering `IDLE` sets both false at [sword.gd:57](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/sword.gd:57).
  - `SETUP` has a `monitoring = true` key at [Sword.tscn:44](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/Sword.tscn:44), but none of the attack animations key `monitoring`.

- The combo buffer opens through the `attack_fast` method key at 0.10 seconds:
  - Animation method keys: [Sword.tscn:149](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/Sword.tscn:149).
  - Times `0.1` and `0.25`: [Sword.tscn:156](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/Sword.tscn:156).
  - The 0.10 key invokes `set_attack_input_listening`, implemented at [sword.gd:91](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/sword.gd:91).
  - Only input received while listening is registered: [sword.gd:71](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/sword.gd:71).

- The next swing may start after the 0.25-second method key invokes `set_ready_for_next_attack`:
  - Method/key association: [Sword.tscn:156](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/Sword.tscn:156).
  - Setter: [sword.gd:96](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/sword.gd:96).
  - Physics starts the next attack once input is registered and readiness is true: [sword.gd:80](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/sword.gd:80).
  - A registered input can also chain at animation completion: [sword.gd:110](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/weapon/sword.gd:110).

- Initial F input pushes the player’s Attack state at [player_state_machine.gd:33](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/player_state_machine.gd:33). Sword completion pops it through [attack.gd:7](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/player/states/combat/attack.gd:7).

The measured constants are at [test_sword_timing.gd:9](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/tests/test_sword_timing.gd:9), and every-frame recording is at [test_sword_timing.gd:133](<scratch>/ch10/walker-2d-finite-state-machine/godot/tests/test_sword_timing.gd:133).

### ANOMALIES

- Swing 2 starts `attack_fast` at animation position `0.25`, travels `0°`, and finishes at the original animation’s endpoint. Calling `play("attack_fast")` while that animation is already playing does not restart it.
- Swing 1 begins with rotation `0°`, despite the animation’s time-zero rotation key being `-80°`.
- At 120 Hz, the 0.10 and 0.25 method keys are observed on frames 14 and 32, rather than the nominal frames 12 and 30.
- The hitbox remains enabled for the entire Attack state; there is no narrower animation-keyed damage window.
