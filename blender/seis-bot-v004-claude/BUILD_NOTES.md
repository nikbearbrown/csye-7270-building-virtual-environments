# Build notes: SEIS bot v004 (Claude's model)

`1,809 robot meshes | 1,103,843 triangles (robot) | 2.12 m tall | 51 bones | 24 procedural materials | Blender 5.1.2`

## Summary
The model was built on 2026-10-18 in the source folder `seis-bot-v004-detailed`. This folder is a copy of the finished files with renders, compares and a glb added.

## Numbers I measured (count_scene.py, Blender 5.1.2)
- Standing file: 1,815 mesh objects (1,809 robot + 6 stage), 1,104,093 evaluated triangles including stage, 31 materials, 1 armature, 51 bones, 1 camera, 10 lights.
- Seated file: 1,823 mesh objects, 1,104,989 triangles, 33 materials, 51 bones.
- Robot only (from the source audit, AUDIT.md): 1,809 meshes, 1,103,843 triangles, height 2.1247 m, 24 materials, all meshes bound to one bone at weight 1.0, audit PASS for names, materials, geometry and weights; 36 open-shell meshes by design.
- Seated height 1.3964 m, 13 degenerate faces (within tolerance).
- Counts agree with the source audit within the stage objects.

## Source decisions (from the source README)
- Where the stills and video frames disagree, the video frames were followed.
- Seated pose: elbows at mid-thigh, thighs tilted 4.6 degrees so the pelvis meets the plinth.
- Mesh cleanup removed 9,697 degenerate faces, 1,394 loose vertices and 4 NaN vertices.

## Process
11 analysts read the stills and 4 read the video frames; 8 builders and 8 refiners built regions; three critique-and-conform rounds followed; then assembly, audit and renders. Parts were modelled in headless Blender processes. The source used a Blender add-on socket for assembly (no MCP server registered).

## What is in scripts/
Toolkit from the source `src/` (assemble.py, seated.py, audit_model.py, render scripts, materials, stage, rig), the region modules in `parts/`, and `workflows/conform.js`. Absolute paths were replaced with `/PATH/TO/...` placeholders for this copy, so edit them before running. Pages are generated by `make_pages.py`; `count_scene.py` and `export_glb.py` were run for this folder.

## glb
No glb existed in the source folder. `seis-bot-v004.glb` was exported from the standing blend with `scripts/export_glb.py` (robot meshes and armature only, skinned, 44.6 MB).

## Renders
Final renders are from the source `renders/final/` (Cycles, 2026-10-18 about 06:00), converted to JPEG (<=1600 px tall) with 1100 px copies in renders/web/. I did not re-render. Round 1-5 intermediates were skipped.

## Known limitations

- Colour and finish are off: the references are dark gunmetal/bronze with a large red chest shield and heavy wear, the model is pale glossy blue-teal. The source README says it followed the video frames, but even against those frames the model reads much lighter.
- Proportions are lighter than the references: the upper body is thin and open, the legs are straight near-parallel columns, hands are small closed fists, and there is no pointing hand.
- The neck is short, chest blades are thinner than the broad bands in the frames, and pauldrons lack the silver beak.
- The head is a round egg shape; the frames show a narrower angular head with a dark face mask.
- The workshop set is only a brick wall, floor and window panels, not the full steampunk room.
- The elbow-on-knee pose would need a 52 degree hunch with the supplied limb lengths, so the Thinker rests elbows at mid-thigh; it is a script-solved study, not an animation.
- Materials are procedural and verified in Cycles only. The glb carries flat base colours without textures or trim, and the teal material (192 meshes) has no base colour set in the glb, so it may show as default white or grey in a viewer. The glb was not opened in a viewer.
- 36 meshes are open shells by design, so the model is not guaranteed watertight. No IK, no facial animation, not tested in a game engine, not for 3D printing.
- The .blend files were saved in Blender 5.1.2 and have never been opened in Blender 4.2.
- The 1,809 count excludes stage objects; count_scene.py over the whole scene reports 1,815 mesh objects (standing) and 1,823 (seated) including the stage.
- Reference images are supplied by Bear and are AI-generated; they are not included here except as crops/scaled copies in compare/.
