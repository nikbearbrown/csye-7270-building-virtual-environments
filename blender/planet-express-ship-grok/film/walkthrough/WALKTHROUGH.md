# Walkthrough: Planet Express ship, round 15

Skill: brutalist.art `blender-walkthrough` at commit `a187fab` (image tour; no model file opened). Film source only: [beat_sheet.md](beat_sheet.md), [SHOTLIST.md](SHOTLIST.md), [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). Narrator: the Liam voice.

**Central observation:** a convincing side silhouette and good detail, with a front that is too narrow and pointed and an engine bell that is too big.

**Credits:** model built by Grok Bot (Blender Python, headless). Reference: 12 photos of a toy and Professor Bear's STL kit (used for the round 15 hull fit). Film rendering: Professor Bear, with his own tools.

**Reported stats (README, rev 34748c8):** 206 mesh and curve objects (instanced rivets included); 109,004 triangles; 13 materials; overall 14.5 x 6.4 x 5.1 model units, hull 11.8 long x 3.3 wide x 3.2 high; Blender 4.2.3, headless, Cycles CPU. These describe the source asset; a learner does not need to match them.

**Includes:** hull, red lip, visor, portholes, turret with barrel and antenna, hatch and ladder, vent, fin with logo, wings, engine bell and nozzles, procedural materials.

**Leaves out:** moving parts, rig, interior, animation. Static assembly only.

**Review:** Round 12: Grok Bot self-score 4/10; Professor Bear said 'pretty good, maybe a six out of ten, but it could use another round.' Round 13 skipped the chunkier hull because of Grok Bot's own 'keep what works' instruction; round 14 did the hull. Round 15 (published): Grok Bot self-score 5.5/10 (side 6, front 5, rear 4, top 5.5); front looks too narrow and pointed against the toy photo, engine bell too big. Professor Bear's verdict on round 15 is pending.
