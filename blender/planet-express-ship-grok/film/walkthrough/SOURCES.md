Stats quoted in the films are **reported by the model README** at that revision, not measured from pixels: 206 mesh and curve objects (instanced rivets included); 109,004 triangles; 13 materials; overall 14.5 x 6.4 x 5.1 model units, hull 11.8 long x 3.3 wide x 3.2 high; Blender 4.2.3, headless, Cycles CPU. These describe the source asset; a learner does not need to match them.

Review status: Round 12: Grok Bot self-score 4/10; Professor Bear said 'pretty good, maybe a six out of ten, but it could use another round.' Round 13 skipped the chunkier hull because of Grok Bot's own 'keep what works' instruction; round 14 did the hull. Round 15 (published): Grok Bot self-score 5.5/10 (side 6, front 5, rear 4, top 5.5); front looks too narrow and pointed against the toy photo, engine bell too big.
