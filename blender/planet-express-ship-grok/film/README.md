# Film source: Planet Express ship

The source for two films, made with the brutalist.art skills Codex updated at commit `a187fab`. The films themselves are not in the repo. Professor Bear renders them with his own tools. The narrator is the Liam voice. There are no subtitles.

| Folder | Skill | Film | Beats | Est. runtime |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`build-lesson/`](build-lesson/) | `blender-build` (image-based lesson) | *Build the Planet Express Ship in Blender, from Images*: one way a person could build a similar model from the published images with a coding agent | 19 | ~5.5 min |
| [`walkthrough/`](walkthrough/) | `blender-walkthrough` (image tour) | *The Planet Express Ship, Walked Through*: the finished model, view by view, with reported stats, the review and its limits | 14 | ~2.8 min |

`build-lesson/` contains `BUILD-LESSON.md`, `PROMPTS.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `SHOTLIST.md`, `beat_sheet.json`/`.md` and `QC.md`. `walkthrough/` contains `WALKTHROUGH.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `SHOTLIST.md`, `beat_sheet.json`/`.md` and `QC.md`.

These files replace the earlier `blender-modelbuild` beat sheet (`beat_sheet.json`, `beat_sheet.md`, `modelbuild-evidence.json`), which has been removed. Runtimes are estimated from word count; the measured narration sets the real clock.
