## B06 · secondary forms · body · ~18.2 s

> Checkpoint four: the neck and pylons that connect everything. Use rounded beams, made with a Bevel modifier, and put each part's origin where it attaches. In this model the neck is barely visible, so make yours thick enough to read in the side view.
