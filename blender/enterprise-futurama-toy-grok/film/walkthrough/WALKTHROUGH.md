# Walkthrough: Futurama-style toy Enterprise, round 9

Skill: brutalist.art `blender-walkthrough` at commit `a187fab` (image tour; no model file opened). Film source only: [beat_sheet.md](beat_sheet.md), [SHOTLIST.md](SHOTLIST.md), [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). Narrator: the Liam voice.

**Central observation:** a fun, recognisable toy reading of the Enterprise whose saucer is too thin, secondary hull too small and neck barely visible.

**Credits:** model built by Grok Bot (Blender Python, headless) from Professor Bear's blueprint images and a Grok Bot concept image he approved. Film rendering: Professor Bear, with his own tools.

**Reported stats (README, rev 34748c8):** 266 mesh objects in the GLB (curves converted; rivets and portholes separate); 155,640 triangles; Blender 4.2.3, headless, Cycles CPU with Open Image Denoise. These describe the source asset; a learner does not need to match them.

**Includes:** saucer with porthole rings and red edge, glass bridge dome, neck, secondary hull with deflector, pylons, two nacelles with caps, rings and stripe panels; procedural materials.

**Leaves out:** moving parts, rig, interior, animation; not a faithful replica.

**Review:** Professor Bear said about the Grok Bot concept image: 'I love it. It's fun... you definitely recognize the Enterprise, but it's not the Enterprise.' Grok Bot's self-score on round 9 is about 6/10 (hero 6, each blueprint view 5). Misses: saucer too thin, secondary hull too small, neck barely visible, bumper lights look like tiles. Professor Bear's verdict on round 9 is pending.
