Stats quoted in the films are **reported by the model README** at that revision, not measured from pixels: 266 mesh objects in the GLB (curves converted; rivets and portholes separate); 155,640 triangles; Blender 4.2.3, headless, Cycles CPU with Open Image Denoise. These describe the source asset; a learner does not need to match them.

Review status: Professor Bear said about the Grok Bot concept image: 'I love it. It's fun... you definitely recognize the Enterprise, but it's not the Enterprise.' Grok Bot's self-score on round 9 is about 6/10 (hero 6, each blueprint view 5). Misses: saucer too thin, secondary hull too small, neck barely visible, bumper lights look like tiles. Professor Bear's verdict on round 9 is pending.
