# SEIS AI Lab engineer robot, body study v002

*An engineer version of the SEIS robot with armoured legs, a connector arm, a wrist display and a red Lato Bold 6. A design study, not a final model.*

`230 meshes · 67,496 triangles · 12 materials in the GLB · 24 bones · mouth shape keys Speaking and Emphasis · 6 spool caps · 2.84 m tall with antenna in the source's audit · Blender 5.1.2`

The second SEIS robot study: the v001 box head, raised eyes, waveform mouth and a bent, broken-mast antenna on a new tapered body with layered chest, narrow upper legs and flared white shin armour, in white, black, gray, Northeastern red and gold accents. The chest 6 is an extruded Lato Bold mesh. The robot's right arm (viewer's left) is a prototype instrument: retained folding fingers, a split palm, a solid audio-style connector with insulating rings, six spool caps and a wrist display with scrolling task text. The source calls it a "visual and mechanical design prototype" and says it is not a functional system. This is a design study, not a final model.

## How to import into Blender

- **`.blend`:** open it in Blender 4.2 or later (File > Open). Saved by Blender 5.1.2; it has never been opened in Blender 4.2, so 4.2 compatibility is untested (use Blender 5.1 or later to match the build). The wrist text scrolls through timeline playback and the connector uses Blender drivers; neither carries into the GLB.
- **`.glb`:** in Blender, File > Import > glTF 2.0 (.glb/.gltf), or drop it into a game engine or a web viewer.

## Honest limitations

- This is a design study, not a final model.
- Likeness to the supplied photographs is weak; the body is still close to the original SEIS robot (the v003 README says so in its own words).
- The right arm is a visual concept only: cable payout, programmed iris states, quarantine behaviour, live agent connections and voice-driven face animation are not implemented. The wrist display is schematic text, not live agent data.
- The source's own prototype proportions are not manufacturing dimensions; the connector travels 0.24 m in the audit.
- Rigid FK posing only; no IK, no animation clips in the GLB, no engine import test.
- Counts: my count_scene run reports 231 mesh objects and 67,498 triangles, which is the source's 230 meshes and 67,496 triangles plus a 2-triangle floor plane; 13 materials in the .blend against 12 in the GLB.
- The 2.84 m height (with antenna) is from the source's own audit; my count cannot give it because of the large floor plane. At this height the robot is large compared with the 1.68 m of v001.
- I did not copy the 24 readout frame PNGs or the review.html; the 4-second mp4 is included.
